    1.  Tips

[Pay] Lose ALL of your Gold and skip the fight.

[Fight!] Enter a combat against three bandits (Pointy, Romeo, and Bear). Defeating them rewards you with cards, 25-35 gold, and the relic Red Mask.

Preserved Insect, Sling of Courage, Slaver's Collar and Black Star have no effect.

## Byrd
stacking_behavior = Intensity
Byrds are weak enemies found in Act 2 that begin each battle with a flying buff. Until each Byrd is dealt attack damage three times (four times on Ascension 17+) in one turn, it will take half damage from all attacks. This combined with its Strength scaling and multi-hit attacks make the Byrd a fearsome foe for some decks.

    1.  Pattern
Can only use Peck or Caw on the first turn with a 62.5% chance for Peck and 37.5% chance for Caw. Afterwards, has a 50% chance of using Peck, 20% chance of using Swoop, and 30% chance of using Caw. Cannot use Peck three times in a row and cannot use Caw or Swoop twice in a row.

Because Byrds have the Flying buff, they will only take half of the damage dealt until they are hit three(or four on Ascension 17+) times in a turn. Here is the sequence of events when a Byrd loses its Flying buffs:
1.  It will get stunned instantly, interrupting its previous intent;
1.  It will use Headbutt the following turn;
1.  It will use Fly.
1.  Returns to its original pattern when Flying.

    1.  Tips
-  Because Byrds attack so many times in one turn and have low HP, Thorns is very powerful in this fight.
-  Attacks that hit multiple enemies multiple times, such as Dagger Spray, are very useful for removing the Byrds' Flying buff.
-  It is more important to attack a Byrd if it is buffing its Strength; its next turn will be much stronger unless it is knocked down or killed.
-  Indirect forms of damage, such as from the Defect's orbs or Poison, ignore the Byrds' Flying buff and are therefore more powerful in this fight.
-  If a Byrd is dealt attack damage more than enough times to knock it down with a single card, the Byrd will be knocked down after the card's effects are finished. Thus, using Whirlwind with 5 Energy will deal half damage to all Byrds 5 times.

    1.  Trivia
-  The name "Byrd" is just "Bird" spelled with a "y".

## Centurion & Mystic
1.  Centurion

    1.  Pattern
If the Mystic is alive, has a 65% chance of using Slash and a 35% chance of using Defend every turn.

If the Mystic is dead, Defend is replaced with Fury.

    1.  Tips
This fight can be simplified greatly by focusing damage on the Centurion. If you can survive his attacks and deal enough damage to overcome the healing output by the Mystic, the Centurion will simply rotate between his weaker attack and defending, allowing you to kill him first. ## Darkling
stacking_behavior = None
Darklings are an Act 3 encounter. The player will fight 3 Darklings at a time, and they all start with the Life Link buff.

    1.  Pattern
On the first turn, all Darklings have a 50% chance of using Nip and a 50% chance of using Harden. The middle Darkling continues this pattern afterwards; it cannot use Chomp.

After the first turn, the outer 2 Darklings have a 30% chance of using Nip, 40% chance of using Chomp and 30% chance of using Harden.

Harden cannot be used twice in a row, Chomp cannot be used twice in a row and Nip cannot be used three times in a row.

After the HP of a Darkling reaches 0, the Darkling can no longer be targeted and Regrow will interrupt its current intent. It will use Reincarnate on the next turn.

## The Maw
starting_buff = NoneThe Maw is an Act 3 encounter. Its scaling over time both through buffing and its attacks increasing in intensity combined with its big health pool can make it a threat for decks with poor offense. Its scaling takes a particularly long time to ramp up to dangerous levels, however.

    1.  Pattern
Always starts with Roar.

After using Roar or Drool, the Maw has a 50/50 chance of using Slam or Nom its next turn.

After using Slam, the Maw has an 50/50 chance of using Drool or Nom its next turn.

After using Nom, the Maw will always use Drool.

## Writhing Mass
The Writhing Mass is an enemy encountered in Act 3. It is unique in that it is the only enemy that can permanently add a card to the player's deck.

stacking_behavior = Intensity

stacking_behavior = None

    1.  Pattern
The Writhing Mass has an equal chance of using Multi-Strike, Strong Strike, Flail, or Wither on the first turn.

Afterwards, it has a 10% chance of using Implant, 30% chance of using Flail, 20% chance of using Wither, 30% chance of using Multi-Strike and 10% chance of using Strong Strike every turn. These chances also apply when changing intent due to its innate Reactive buff.

The Writhing Mass can use Implant only once per combat and cannot use a move twice in a row*; additionally, its intent will always* change when Reactive is activated.

- Due to the coding of Writhing Mass, after Implant was used, when the current intent is Strong Strike, there is a 1 in 90 chance that the next intent is also Strong Strike, tested 7/30/2025.
