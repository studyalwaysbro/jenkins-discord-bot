## Technology
Tech Level Description]]

Tech defines the level of development (the highest level of research bench you can build). New levels allow the player to build new structures and research further. Characters with higher science skill will research new tech faster. Technology falls into 8 different categories, with 6 different levels, the higher-level permits more advanced technology in their respective category. Some tech can only be learned from blueprints. For a complete overview and a technology tree go here. For a category page of everything relating to technology go here.

## Hydroponics (Tech)
version number = 1.0.68new_research = Hydroponic Hemp, Hydroponic Riceweed, Hydroponic Vegetables, Hydroponic Wheatstrawnew_building(s) = Hydroponic Hempnew_building(s) = Hydroponic Riceweednew_building(s) = Hydroponic Vegetablesnew_building(s) = Hydroponic WheatstrawIn-game description

After researching Hydroponics, characters can research how to make hydroponic growing stations for four different types of crops. Only Hemp, Riceweed, Greenfruit, and Wheatstraw can be grown with hydroponics. Hydroponics Bays are smaller than outdoor farms but can allow for bases to be built in any area without concern for what Environment it is.

Hydroponic farms require Water and Power to grow. If there is not enough power, farms can pause growth or grow at a decreased rate.

After completing all the research, characters can build Hydroponics with 4 Iron Plates, 2 Electrical Components, and 10 of their desired crop.

## Tech Level (Tech)
version number = 1.0.68

new_research = Tech Level 4
In-game description
new_research = Tech Level 5
new_research = Tech Level 6
requirements = Tech Level 5

Tech Levels serve as a gating mechanism to other Technologies. They ensure that a player must research in tiers before reaching late game technological wonders such as Hydroponics or Automated Mining Tech.

Players are required to begin their research with a Small Research Bench at Tech Level I. Progression to Tech Level II unlocks Research Bench II, and so forth. These advancements are made by researching the next tier and having the appropriate materials. Books are the only requirement initially for many forms of research. Greater levels require rarer items such as Ancient Science Books or AI Cores.
