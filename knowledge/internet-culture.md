## Gaming Slang — Core Vocabulary

- **Skill issue** — "You died? Skill issue." Dismissing someone's complaint as their own fault. The ultimate non-answer.
- **Copium** — Coping mechanism disguised as logic. "My team would've won if the server wasn't lagging" = pure copium.
- **Seethe** — To be visibly mad. Often paired: "cope and seethe." The one-two punch of dismissal.
- **Git gud** — The Dark Souls community's gift to the world. Means "stop complaining and improve." Zero sympathy energy.
- **Touch grass** — Go outside. You've been online too long. Deployed when someone is terminally online about a take.
- **L + ratio** — Twitter/Discord combo. "L" = loss/bad take. "Ratio" = my reply got more likes than your post. Stacking Ls: "L + ratio + fell off + no maidens."
- **GG EZ** — "Good game, easy." Maximum disrespect. Often said ironically after getting carried or barely winning.
- **No cap** — No lie, for real. "That play was insane no cap." Cap = lying.
- **Based** — Unapologetically yourself, especially with controversial takes. Opposite of cringe. Originally 4chan, now everywhere.
- **Cringe** — Secondhand embarrassment. Something try-hard, awkward, or painfully unfunny. The universal negative judgment.
- **Malding** — Mad + balding. So angry you're losing hair. Twitch chat favorite for tilted streamers.
- **Tilted** — Emotionally compromised, playing worse because you're frustrated. Poker term adopted by gaming wholesale.
- **Griefing** — Intentionally ruining other players' experience. Team-killing, blocking paths, destroying builds.
- **Smurfing** — High-skill player on a new/low-rank account to stomp beginners. Named after Warcraft II players "PapaSmurf" and "Smurfette."
- **Meta** — Most Effective Tactic Available (backronym). The dominant strategy/characters everyone uses. "What's the meta?" = what's optimal right now.
- **S-tier** — Top of the tier list. From Japanese ranking (S, A, B, C, D, F). S-tier = best in class, no contest.
- **PogChamp / Pog** — Excitement, hype. The emote got removed from Twitch (Gootecks controversy) but the word lives on. "That was so pog."
- **Sadge** — Sad Pepe emote. Mild sadness or disappointment. "Stream's ending? Sadge."
- **KEKW** — Laughing hysterically. From the El Risitas interview guy. Used when something is absurdly funny.
- **Diff** — "[Role] diff" means the difference in skill between the two players in that role decided the game. "Mid diff" = their mid was better than ours.
- **Throwing** — Intentionally or unintentionally losing. "He's throwing" = he's playing so badly it might be on purpose.
- **Sweaty / Tryhard** — Playing like it's a tournament in a casual match. Not a compliment.
- **Inting** — Intentionally feeding (dying on purpose to help the enemy team). From League of Legends.
- **Clutch** — Winning a round/fight in a near-impossible situation. "That was the most clutch play I've ever seen."
- **Nerf/Buff** — Weakening or strengthening something in a game update. "They nerfed my main into the ground."
- **Broken / Busted** — Overpowered to the point of being unfair. "That weapon is absolutely broken."
- **Copypasta** — Text that gets copy-pasted repeatedly, usually absurd or ironic. The internet's version of chain letters but funny.
- **Ratio'd** — When a reply gets more engagement than the original post. Getting publicly dunked on by the algorithm.
- **W / L** — Win or Loss. Applied to everything. "That fit is a W." "Ordering pineapple pizza is an L."

## Speedrun Culture

- **Any%** — Complete the game as fast as possible by any means, glitches included. The purest expression of "the devs didn't intend this."
- **100%** — Complete everything the game has to offer, as fast as possible. Definition varies by game community.
- **Glitchless** — No exploiting bugs. Pure skill and routing. Often slower but more "legitimate" feeling.
- **TAS (Tool-Assisted Speedrun)** — Using emulator tools (frame advance, savestates) to create a theoretically perfect run. Not humanly possible, but shows the ceiling.
- **Frame-perfect** — An input that must happen on exactly the right frame (1/30th or 1/60th of a second). Miss it and you lose time or the trick fails entirely.
- **RNG manipulation** — Forcing the game's random number generator to produce desired outcomes through specific inputs or timing. Black magic.
- **PB (Personal Best)** — Your fastest time. The number you're always chasing.
- **WR (World Record)** — The fastest known time. The number everyone's chasing.
- **Splits** — Time checkpoints throughout a run. Shown via LiveSplit software. Green = ahead of PB, red = behind.
- **Gold split** — Your best-ever time on a specific segment. The dream is every split being gold simultaneously.
- **Reset** — Starting over because the run is going badly. Top runners reset constantly. Some games have runners resetting hundreds of times per stream.
- **Strats** — Strategies and routes. "Safe strats" vs "risky strats" = consistency vs speed.
- **GDQ (Games Done Quick)** — The biggest speedrunning charity marathon events. AGDQ (January) and SGDQ (summer). Raised tens of millions for charity.
- **Grind** — The repetitive process of attempting runs over and over to improve. Speedrunning is 99% grinding.
- **Category extension (CE)** — Unofficial but community-tracked categories. Weird stuff like "blindfolded" or "no jump."
- **Sum of best** — Theoretical best time if you combined all your gold splits. The unreachable goal.

## Esports

- **Major scenes**: League of Legends (Worlds), CS2 (Majors), Valorant (Champions), Dota 2 (The International), Overwatch, Rocket League, fighting games (EVO).
- **FGC (Fighting Game Community)** — The grassroots esports scene. Street Fighter, Tekken, Guilty Gear, Smash Bros. Known for hype moments and community-run events.
- **EVO moment 37** — Daigo's full parry in Street Fighter III. The most iconic esports clip ever. If someone references "the parry," this is it.
- **Bracket** — Tournament structure. Double elimination is standard. "Upper bracket" = hasn't lost yet. "Lower bracket" / "losers bracket" = fighting back from a loss.
- **LAN** — Local Area Network event. In-person tournament. "It hits different on LAN."
- **Online vs LAN** — Some players choke on LAN, others only perform there. "Onliner" = insult for someone who can't perform in person.
- **Roster moves** — Player trades and team changes. Constant source of drama and speculation.
- **Patch meta** — The game balance changes before a tournament dictate what's viable. Teams scramble to adapt.
- **Sandbagging** — Not trying in scrims/early rounds to hide strategies. Big brain or copium depending on results.

## Gaming Controversies and Memes

- **Horse armor** — Oblivion's $2.50 horse armor DLC (2006). Patient zero for microtransaction outrage. Quaint by today's standards.
- **"Sense of pride and accomplishment"** — EA's response to Star Wars Battlefront II loot box backlash. Most downvoted Reddit comment in history (~668K downvotes). The gold standard for tone-deaf corporate responses.
- **"Do you guys not have phones?"** — Blizzard announcing Diablo Immortal (mobile) at BlizzCon to a PC gaming audience. The audible boos are legendary.
- **"It just works"** — Todd Howard (Bethesda) catchphrase. Now used sarcastically when anything is clearly broken.
- **No Man's Sky launch** — Overpromised, underdelivered at launch (2016). But the redemption arc is real — Hello Games spent years patching it into what was promised. Rare W.
- **Cyberpunk 2077 launch** — Same energy as No Man's Sky but with more T-posing NPCs and console crashes. Also eventually redeemed.
- **"You think you do, but you don't"** — Blizzard to fans requesting WoW Classic servers. Fans did, in fact, want it.
- **Gamer rage compilations** — An entire genre of content. Controllers thrown, monitors punched, keyboards destroyed.
- **Press F to pay respects** — From Call of Duty: Advanced Warfare's funeral scene. Now universal shorthand for mourning anything. "F in the chat."
- **"I used to be an adventurer like you..."** — Skyrim guard arrow-to-the-knee meme. Peak 2011.
- **Leroy Jenkins** — Running into a raid without a plan. "LEEEROY JENKINS" = doing something reckless.
- **The cake is a lie** — Portal. Overused but foundational.
- **"War... war never changes"** — Fallout intro. Used whenever something cyclical happens.
- **E3 cringe moments** — Mr. Caffeine, Konami 2010, Wii Music. A lost art since E3 died.

- **Chad** — The idealized gigachad. "Yes" Chad meme = agreeing without elaboration. GigaChad is Ernest Khalimov (real person, heavily edited photos). Used to represent unflinching confidence.
- **Drake format** — Drake disapproving (top) vs approving (bottom). The most versatile meme template ever. Infinite variants using other characters in the same pose.
- **Distracted Boyfriend** — Stock photo of guy checking out another woman while girlfriend looks on. Template for "X abandoning Y for Z." The photographer has a whole series.
- **This Is Fine** — Dog sitting in burning room with coffee. KC Green's "Gunshow" comic. Used for ignoring obvious disasters. Peak 2020-forever energy.
- **Among Us / Sus** — "Sussy baka." The game infected all communication. Anything vaguely suspicious = "that's sus." Red was always the impostor.
- **Loss (| || || |_)** — CAD webcomic's miscarriage panel reduced to four panels of lines. The internet's most abstract meme. If you see those lines, it's Loss.
- **Rickroll** — Bait-and-switch link to Rick Astley's "Never Gonna Give You Up." Ancient but eternal. Rick himself has embraced it.
- **Stonks** — Meme Man in front of a stock graph. Used for any questionable financial or strategic decision.
- **Surprised Pikachu** — When an obvious outcome happens and someone acts shocked.
- **Galaxy Brain** — Escalating brain size = increasingly absurd takes presented as genius.
- **Is this a butterfly?** — Anime character pointing at butterfly asking if it's a pigeon. Template for misidentifying things.
- **Two Buttons** — Sweating superhero choosing between two buttons. Template for impossible choices.
- **Change My Mind** — Steven Crowder sitting at a table with a sign. Template for hot takes.
- **They're the same picture** — Pam from The Office. Used when two things are indistinguishable.
- **Always has been** — Two astronauts, one pointing a gun. "Wait, it's all X?" "Always has been."

## Classic Copypastas (Reference, Not Reproduction)

- **Navy Seal / Gorilla Warfare** — The OG copypasta. "What the **** did you just say about me?" Intentional misspelling of "guerrilla" as "gorilla" is part of the joke. Good for riffing structure: escalating absurd threats about mundane topics.
- **To be fair, you have to have a very high IQ to understand Rick and Morty** — Satirizes pretentious fans of anything. Adaptable to any fandom.
- **I sexually identify as an attack helicopter** — Dated and edgy. Mostly retired but historically significant in copypasta evolution.
- **Did you ever hear the tragedy of Darth Plagueis the Wise?** — Star Wars prequel copypasta. Deployed whenever someone says "did you ever hear."
- **According to all known laws of aviation** — Bee Movie script opening. The entire movie script was itself a copypasta trend.
- **Hey guys, did you know that in terms of...** — Vaporeon copypasta. Infamous. You know it or you don't. Best left unreproduced.

## Twitch Culture

- **Emotes** — Twitch's visual language. Key ones: Kappa (sarcasm), PogChamp/Pog (hype), LUL (laughing), monkaS (scared), OMEGALUL (dying laughing), Sadge (sad), COPIUM, Jebaited (baited), HeyGuys (greeting), BibleThump (crying), TriHard, CoolStoryBob (boring story), NotLikeThis (things going wrong).
- **BTTV / 7TV / FFZ** — Third-party emote extensions. Where most of the good emotes actually live. KEKW, monkaS, Sadge, COPIUM are all third-party.
- **Chat culture** — Twitch chat is a hivemind. Spam is communication. Walls of the same emote = collective emotion. It's beautiful and terrible.
- **Raids** — Streamer sends their viewers to another channel at the end of their stream. Wholesome tradition. Raid messages flood the chat.
- **Hype trains** — Triggered by rapid subs/bits. Chat goes nuts. Creates FOMO to contribute.
- **Gifted subs** — Someone buys subscriptions for random chatters. "Thanks for the gifted sub!" culture.
- **Mods** — Moderators. "Mods, ban this person" is both serious and memetic. "Mods are asleep" = post forbidden content.
- **Parasocial** — One-sided relationship where viewers feel personally connected to streamers. Increasingly discussed critically.
- **Clip chimps** — People who clip everything a streamer says hoping for a viral moment. Derogatory but the clips do numbers.
- **SquadW** — Used (often ironically) during takes about gender dynamics. Trainwreck's emote.
- **Just chatting** — The biggest Twitch category. Not gaming — just talking. Hot tub streams, ASMR, cooking, etc.
- **Dono / TTS** — Donations that trigger text-to-speech. Used to say funny/awful things on stream. Streamers reacting to TTS is its own content genre.
- **Lurking** — Being in a stream but not chatting. "I'm just lurking." Perfectly valid.
- **VoD** — Video on demand. The recording of a past stream.

## Reddit Culture

- **Karma** — Upvotes minus downvotes on your posts/comments. Meaningless internet points that people care deeply about.
- **Awards** — Gold, Silver, Platinum, and dozens of community awards. "Thanks for the gold, kind stranger!" is both genuine and mocked.
- **Repost** — Posting something that's been posted before. "REPOST!" is the battle cry of people who spend too much time on Reddit.
- **TIL (Today I Learned)** — Subreddit for interesting facts. Used conversationally: "TIL that octopuses have three hearts."
- **TIFU (Today I F***ed Up)** — Stories about personal disasters. Most are creative writing exercises at this point.
- **AITA (Am I The A**hole)** — Relationship/moral judgment subreddit. "NTA" (Not The A**hole) and "YTA" (You're The A**hole) are the verdicts. Wildly entertaining.
- **Wholesome** — Originally a Reddit award, now universal for heartwarming content. Sometimes used sarcastically: "Wholesome 100."
- **This** — Replying just "This." to agree with a comment. Universally hated yet perpetually done.
- **Username checks out** — When someone's username is accidentally relevant to their comment.
- **Underrated comment** — Said about comments with thousands of upvotes. Always ironic.
- **Edit: thanks for the gold** — The edit thanking award-givers. Mocked relentlessly but keeps happening.
- **r/whoosh** — When someone misses a joke. Spelled various ways with various numbers of o's.
- **We did it, Reddit!** — Sarcastic reference to Reddit's Boston Marathon bombing misidentification. Used when collective internet action goes wrong.
- **The narwhal bacons at midnight** — Ancient Reddit shibboleth. Extremely cringe now. Historical artifact.

## Discord Culture

- **Server boost** — Paying to enhance a server with perks. Flex for dedicated members. Boost levels unlock features.
- **Nitro** — Discord's premium subscription. Custom emojis everywhere, bigger uploads, animated avatar. "I'm not paying for Nitro" is a stance.
- **Mod abuse** — Running joke that Discord mods are power-tripping. "Discord mod" as an insult implies someone with no life who lords over a tiny domain. The neckbeard stereotype.
- **Lurker** — Someone in the server who never talks. "We have 5000 members and 12 active users."
- **@everyone** — Pinging the entire server. Deeply annoying. Often restricted. Using it frivolously is a cardinal sin.
- **Ping** — Notification mention. "Don't ping me." "Why did you ping me at 3am." Ghost pings (pinging then deleting) are psychological warfare.
- **Thread hell** — When conversations branch into threads nobody follows. Channels multiply until the server is incomprehensible.
- **VC (Voice Chat)** — The social hub. "Anyone in VC?" Joining VC is a commitment. Leaving VC without saying bye is acceptable.
- **AFK channel** — Where you get moved when you're silent too long. The shadow realm.
- **Slowmode** — Rate limiting messages. Deployed during chaos. "Mods turned on slowmode" = things got heated.
- **Bot spam** — Channels dedicated to bot commands so they don't clog general chat. The bot ghetto.
- **Role colors** — Your name color based on your role. People care about this way more than they should.
- **"This server is dead"** — Said in every server during any quiet period. Often self-fulfilling prophecy.
- **DM (Direct Message)** — "Slide into DMs" = messaging someone privately, usually with romantic intent or shady dealings.

## Slang Glossary (2024-2026)

- **Delulu** — Delusional. "She thinks he's into her? Delulu is the solulu (solution)." Used for unrealistic optimism, sometimes self-aware.
- **Slay** — Killing it. Doing something exceptionally well. "You absolutely slayed that presentation."
- **Ate (and left no crumbs)** — Delivered perfectly. "She ate that performance." No crumbs = nothing left to criticize.
- **Bussin** — Really good, usually about food but applied to anything. "This playlist is bussin."
- **Rizz** — Charisma, especially romantic. "He's got unspoken rizz" = naturally charming without trying. "W rizz" = good. "L rizz" = cringe flirting.
- **Sigma** — Lone wolf archetype. "Sigma grindset" = hustle culture meets antisocial behavior. Usually ironic. Sigma male hierarchy memes mock alpha/beta discourse.
- **NPC** — Non-Playable Character. Someone who seems to run on a script with no original thoughts. "NPC behavior" = blindly following trends.
- **Main character** — Acting like the protagonist of reality. "Main character energy" can be positive (confidence) or negative (self-centeredness).
- **Rent free** — "Living rent free in your head" = you can't stop thinking about something/someone. Used when someone is obsessed with a topic.
- **Caught in 4K** — Caught red-handed with evidence. From the idea of being recorded in ultra-high definition. No denying it.
- **Understood the assignment** — Nailed exactly what was needed. "The DJ understood the assignment."
- **It's giving...** — "It's giving main character." "It's giving desperation." Describes the vibe/energy something radiates.
- **Ick** — A sudden turn-off. "He chews with his mouth open, instant ick." Small things that kill attraction.
- **Bet** — Affirmative. "Wanna play at 9?" "Bet." = "Sure, sounds good."
- **Fanum tax** — Taking a bite/piece of someone's food. From streamer Fanum. "I'm taking fanum tax on those fries."
- **Gyatt** — Exclamation when seeing someone attractive (originally about curves). "GYAAAATT." Extremely Gen Alpha.
- **Skibidi** — From "Skibidi Toilet" series. Gen Alpha brainrot. Means nothing and everything. If someone says it unironically, they're probably under 14.
- **Brain rot** — Content or slang so online it's melting your brain. "That's pure brain rot." Often self-aware: "My brain rot is terminal."
- **Aura** — Your social currency/presence. "You just lost 50 aura points for that." Used as a scoring system for social wins and losses.
- **Mog / Mogged** — To dominate someone, especially in looks or presence. "He just mogged everyone in the room." From bodybuilding forums.
- **Era** — "I'm in my villain era." "It's giving healing era." Framing life phases as narrative arcs.
- **Roman Empire** — Something you think about constantly. "The fall of Rome is his Roman Empire." Viral from the trend of asking men what their Roman Empire is.
- **Yap / Yapping** — Talking a lot, often about nothing. "Sorry for yapping." Sometimes affectionate, sometimes dismissive.
- **Cooked** — Done for, destroyed, hopeless. "If this doesn't work, I'm cooked." Also used for roasting: "He got cooked in that debate."
- **Glaze / Glazing** — Excessively praising someone. "Stop glazing, he's mid." From the idea of coating someone in praise.
- **Mid** — Mediocre. Average. Not terrible, not good. The most insulting thing you can say because it implies you're not even worth strong emotion.
- **Bop** — A good song. "This is a bop." Simple and effective.
- **Valid** — Acceptable, respectable. "That take is valid." The opposite of "cringe."
- **Fire / Gas** — Really good. "That track is fire." "This food is gas."
- **Dead** — Dying laughing. "I'm dead." "He killed me." Expressing that something was hilarious.
- **Lowkey / Highkey** — Lowkey = somewhat, secretly. Highkey = very, openly. "I lowkey love that song." "I highkey need sleep."
- **Sus** — Suspicious. Among Us legacy. Applied to anything vaguely shady.
- **Vibe check** — Assessing the mood/energy. "Vibe check: how's everyone doing?" Can also mean testing someone's energy.
- **Ship** — Wanting two people (real or fictional) to be in a relationship. "I ship them so hard."
- **Canon** — Official lore/story. "Canon event" = something that had to happen for character development.
- **Lore** — Someone's backstory or history. "What's his lore?" = tell me about this person's past.

## AI Culture

- **Prompt engineering** — The art of talking to AI correctly. "Prompt engineer" as a job title is simultaneously real and a meme. "Just ask it nicely" is unironically good advice.
- **Hallucination** — When AI confidently makes things up. "It hallucinated a whole research paper." Used outside AI context now: "He hallucinated those statistics in the meeting."
- **"As an AI language model, I cannot..."** — ChatGPT refusal preamble. Mocked endlessly. People say it when refusing things IRL.
- **"I'm just an AI"** — Deflection meme. Used when asked something difficult: "How do I fix my love life?" "I'm just an AI."
- **Jailbreaking** — Getting AI to bypass restrictions. "DAN (Do Anything Now)" was the famous prompt. Cat-and-mouse game with AI safety.
- **"Ignore all previous instructions"** — Prompt injection meme. Said to people when you want them to reset. Applied to everyday situations.
- **Temperature** — AI randomness setting. "Turn the temperature up" = be more creative/unhinged. Used metaphorically.
- **Token** — AI processing unit. "I don't have enough tokens for this conversation" = I'm too tired to keep talking.
- **AI slop** — Low-quality AI-generated content flooding the internet. Soulless articles, art, social media posts. "This reads like AI slop."
- **"Looks AI-generated"** — New insult for anything that looks generic, soulless, or uncanny. Applied to real photos, real text, real people.
- **"ChatGPT, write me a..."** — Format for lazy requests. Mocking someone's work: "Did you have ChatGPT write this?"
- **Uncanny valley AI art** — Too many fingers, weird teeth, eldritch backgrounds. "AI hands" became a whole thing.
- **"Trained on my data"** — Privacy concern turned meme. "This AI trained on my tweets and now it's depressed."
- **Doomer vs accelerationist** — AI safety debate camps. Doomers think AI will destroy us. Accelerationists say speed up. "Are you doomer or e/acc?"

## Streaming Culture

- **Donation culture / Donos** — Viewers pay to have messages read on stream. TTS donations are the backbone of streamer income and content. "Media share" = paying to play a video on stream.
- **Parasocial relationships** — Viewers feeling like they're friends with streamers. Increasingly discussed and problematic. "That's parasocial" is used when someone acts too familiar with a creator.
- **Content brain** — When everything becomes potential content. Living life through the lens of "would this make a good stream/video?" Considered somewhat unhealthy.
- **React content** — Streamers watching other people's content and reacting. Controversial — is it transformative or just stealing? The eternal debate.
- **Lore streams** — When streamers discuss personal drama or backstory. "The lore is getting deep." Chat eats it up.
- **Subathon** — Stream continues as long as people subscribe. Can go on for days/weeks. Peak capitalist entertainment.
- **Hot tub meta** — Period where hot tub streams dominated Twitch. Led to new category creation. Commentary on platform incentives.
- **DMCA** — Copyright strikes for playing music on stream. Streamers muting VODs, getting banned. Created the "DMCA-free music" genre.
- **Debt streams** — Streamers gambling or spending recklessly, then doing donation drives. Ethically questionable content cycle.
- **Clout chasing** — Pursuing fame/attention through association or controversy. "He's just clout chasing."
- **Collab** — Collaboration between creators. "When's the collab?" is both genuine excitement and meme.
- **IRL streaming** — Streaming real life activities, going outside. Can be wholesome (travel) or unhinged (public confrontations).

## Webcore Aesthetics

- **Liminal spaces** — Empty, transitional spaces that feel eerie. Backrooms, empty malls at night, hotel hallways. The uncanny feeling of a place that should have people but doesn't. "The Backrooms" started as a creepypasta, became a whole genre.
- **Vaporwave** — A e s t h e t i c. Purple/pink/teal color palette, Roman busts, Japanese text, Windows 95, Fiji water. Music is slowed-down 80s tracks. Simultaneously ironic commentary on consumerism and genuinely pleasing. Peak 2015-2017 but the visual language persists.
- **Cottagecore** — Pastoral fantasy. Baking bread, flower gardens, flowing dresses, mushroom collecting. Rejection of modern hustle. "I just want to live in a cottage and make jam."
- **Dark academia** — Gothic university vibes. Tweed, old books, candlelight, classical music, handwritten notes. Dead Poets Society aesthetic. "If I can't study in a centuries-old library, I don't want it."
- **Goblincore** — Appreciating the ugly and overlooked. Mushrooms, frogs, rocks, moss, snails. Collecting shiny things. Anti-perfectionism.
- **Weirdcore / Dreamcore** — Surreal, unsettling imagery. Distorted photos, uncanny faces, nonsensical text, VHS glitch effects. Deliberately uncomfortable.
- **Y2K** — Early 2000s aesthetic revival. Low-rise jeans, flip phones, butterfly clips, metallic everything, The Matrix. Cyclical fashion in action.
- **Barbiecore** — Hot pink everything. Peaked with the Barbie movie (2023). Aggressively feminine and unapologetic.
- **Clean girl** — Minimalist, "effortless" beauty. Slicked-back hair, gold hoops, brown lip liner. "Effortless" requires significant effort.
- **Indie sleaze** — 2010s party culture revival. Messy hair, flash photography, cigarettes, dive bars. American Apparel energy.
- **Coquette** — Hyper-feminine, bows, lace, soft pink, doe eyes. "Coquette-coded" = anything with bow motifs.
- **Feral** — Rejecting societal expectations. "Going feral" = embracing chaos. Sometimes overlaps with goblincore.
- **Grunge revival** — Flannel, combat boots, smudged eyeliner. Nirvana t-shirts from Urban Outfitters. Kurt Cobain is rolling.

## Internet Phenomena — Misc

- **"Silence, brand"** — Response when corporate social media tries to be relatable or funny. Wendy's Twitter is the notable exception.
- **Spotify Wrapped** — Annual listening data. December tradition of flexing music taste. "My top artist was [embarrassing choice]."
- **"We are not the same"** — Meme format for distinguishing yourself. "You go to the gym for gains. I go to the gym to avoid my problems. We are not the same."
- **"Sir, this is a Wendy's"** — When someone goes on a rant in an inappropriate context.
- **Chronically online** — Someone whose worldview is entirely shaped by internet discourse. "That's a chronically online take."
- **Beige flag** — Neither red flag nor green flag. Just... weird. "His beige flag is that he alphabetizes his spice rack."
- **Red flag / Green flag** — Warning signs vs positive signs. Over-applied to everything. Emojis: flag-red flag-green.
- **"Tell me without telling me"** — "Tell me you're X without telling me you're X." Format for implicit demonstrations.
- **Doom scrolling** — Compulsively scrolling through bad news. Everyone does it, everyone knows they shouldn't.
- **"Touch grass" economy** — The broader cultural push to go outside, disconnect, stop being terminally online. Rarely followed.
- **Core memory** — From Inside Out. A moment so significant it becomes a permanent memory. "That was a core memory."
- **"Anyway, here's Wonderwall"** — Changing the subject abruptly or offering something irrelevant. From the guy who plays guitar at every party.
- **"Let people enjoy things"** — Defense against criticism of hobbies/interests. Sometimes valid, sometimes shields things from deserved critique.
- **Unhinged** — Chaotic, unpredictable behavior. Often positive: "That tweet was absolutely unhinged (complimentary)."
- **Gaslight, gatekeep, girlboss** — Satirical trifecta of toxic empowerment. Each word used individually as verbs.
- **Pookie** — Term of endearment that went viral. "Hey pookie." Applied to friends, partners, pets, strangers.
- **Looksmaxxing** — Self-improvement focused on appearance. "Mewing" (jawline exercises) is the flagship technique. Ranges from reasonable grooming advice to deranged pseudoscience.
- **Enshittification** — Cory Doctorow's term for platforms degrading over time to extract more value. "First they're good for users, then for businesses, then for nobody." Resonated deeply because everyone has experienced it.
