## Campfire
1.  Description
The ' is used to cook food, boil water, create medications, and craft a few resources. It can be found in the many lodges and numerous camp sites scattered all over the map. It can be crafted using 5 Small Stones. To use it, place it down and open by pressing "E".

The  can be activated as long as it has fuel, regardless if it is being used to cook anything or not.

Being with in 2 blocks of the  will grant the Warming Fire buff, which grants +15 Cold Resistance. s may set fire to a character or zombie if they walk through it while it is turned on.

    1.  Crafting
    1.  Required Items

    1.  Usage
Recipes are listed in alphabetical order in the left hand window. The search bar at the top left will filter recipes to include all with the same text in the name. Recipes will automatically turn white in color and sort to the top if the  has the ingredients to make them in the character's inventory.

Recipes may require cookware (see below) to be in the  before they can be crafted; the needed cookware for a given item will display in the top right of the center window. Cookwares are placed in the top right cells to open up additional recipes. Some recipes are gated with books that will turn white when the character has read them.

No recipe will be available until there is fuel in the fuel cells. Fuels are placed in the cells below the tool cells. The  can be turned on either with the on/off button below the fuel, or by starting a new recipe. The  will not turn off automatically unless all the fuel is expended.

Selecting a recipe will display the required materials. Quantity to be crafted can be selected below the item image either by typing in the qty or by pressing the left and right arrows to increase/decrease the amount by one. Pressing the right arrow with the bar will max the qty to the number of available resources from the character. Selecting the "cook" button will activate the fire, remove the materials from the character inventory, place the item in a queue in the bottom left displaying the time to craft all qty, and begin cooking the new items. Up to 4 item types can be queued at a time.

Items will output one at a time per base time until all have been crafted. When the items are completed they will dispense in the 6 output cells in the lower right window. Once one type is complete the queue will automatically move to the next type.

    1.  Meat Comparison

-  Charred Meat, Boiled Meat and Grilled Meat are the three main types of meats you can make at the campfire, each having different pluses and minuses.
-  Charred Meat is considerably easier to acquire than the other two, not needing any ingredients or tools other than the Campfire and some animal flesh.

## Dew Collector
A21.0

    1.  Description
The Dew Collector is a newly added workstation in Alpha 21. As water collection has been rebalanced by removing empty jars & cans from the game, the Dew Collector has been added to provide murky water, or clean drinking water if the water filter mod is installed.
    1.  Crafting

    1. ## Required Items

    1.  Usage
The Dew Collector must be placed with a clear view of the sky, bars and other 'see-through' shapes are acceptable to be placed above. Once placed, the Dew Collector will automatically accumulate Water at a rate of approximately 3 jars every 24 game hours (between 6-10 in-game hours per water). Multiple Dew Collectors can be placed being.

    1.  Tools
The dew collector has space for 3 upgrades, The gatherer, tarp and water filter upgrade can be installed. Each Dew collector can be fitted with one of each upgrade. With all 3 upgrades, 12 clean, drinkable water can be produced per day.

This is especially effective using Yucca Juice Smoothies or drinking open water using a Water Purifier Mod as your main source of hydration as they require no water items to make.

## List of Foods
Food/Cooking is an Item Group.

See Cooking system for more details.

Food is an essential part of 7 Days to Die. Consuming food will add to your Fullness, whilst consuming a drink will add to your Hydration. Some consumables will add to both.

Fullness and Hydration will deplete over time. Physical activities such as mining will deplete Fullness faster and hotter biomes will increase the rate Hydration depletes.

Consuming food and drink does not instantly fill up Fullness/Hydration. The amount provided by the food and drink will display in the bottom left corner and is slowly added to the player. The consumed items stack, for example eating a Grilled Meat will add 10. A second one will add another 10 to the amount and so on.

It is possible to consume more than your max value (100 at the beginning and +1 for every level of the player). Doing so will keep your food/water level full for longer.

Some items will give you a buff on top of the Fullness/Hydration, see tables below.

Most food and drink recipes are unlocked with the Food crafting skill. There are exceptions, such as Blackstrap Coffee, which requires The Art of Mining Vol 4 skill book.
