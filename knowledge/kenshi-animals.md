## Beak Thing
image1 = Beak Thing.JPGBeak Things, also known as Gutters, are among the most dangerous predators in the world and widely feared for their tendency to eat people alive.

They are found in many places, but are mostly grouped into 7 different core regions:

-  Leviathan Coast, with possible migrations to Cannibal Plains and then Purple Sands.
-  Gut and The Unwanted Zone, with both possibly having migrations to Stormgap Coast, Venge and then Shem.
-  Bonefields and likely migrated to their neighboring High Bonefields and Flats Lagoon.
-  Vain
-  The Crater
-  Greenbeach

Their nests can spawn between 1 and 30 of the much prized Beak Thing Eggs. These creatures run extremely fast, hit hard, and will eat incapacitated organic prey whether dead or not. They can also spawn as even more powerful Elders (Age 1.9-2.1) in the High Bonefields and Bonefields. Beak Things have the same min and max speed "skill" (170), so athletics has no impact on how fast they move; their movement speed is solely determined by their age. This results in Elder Beak Things having the highest movement speed of any creature in Kenshi.

As Beak Things are very difficult to outrun, low-level characters who cannot defeat them have limited options to survive an encounter. Luring them into another pack of enemies is sometimes an option or running away while constantly juking. But the best option is not to be noticed by them in the first place. Sneaking past them may be possible for characters with moderate skill, especially at night. If discovered simply breaking line of sight and activating sneak again is usually more than enough to get away.

The Beak Thing's penchant for eating prey alive is alluded to in a bar conversation that can be heard in the United Cities, in which two residents discuss an Anti-Slaver raiding party leaving a victim tied up "for the Gutters." If a unit is knocked into recovery coma and a Beak Thing begins to eat it, their best hope for survival is to have another party member fight it or lure it away, as they rarely stop eating before every body part has been consumed from the bottom up. If not knocked into a recovery coma the character will instantly get back up upon being eaten causing the Beak Thing to flee.

athletics=35

    1.  Dealing with Beak Things
One strategy to kill these creatures is by having one tank/attacker taking the hits, while other members shoot at it with crossbows. If the group lacks ranged weapons, it is wise to surround the Beak Thing from different sides in order to avoid its multiple enemy attack.

The only attack Beak Things have is by swinging their head downwards. It takes quite some time for their attack to strike someone due to their long attack animation, so if a character can move away (NOT sideways, they can turn) from the Beak Thing, they will avoid the attack unharmed. The attack can also be blocked if the block is made for upwards attack.

## Blood Spider
type = roaming, nesting
Blood Spiders are horrible, disgusting little insectoids whose menacing hiss will haunt your nightmares. Despite their blood-red coloring, they can be hard to see as they are stick-thin and primarily patrol shadowy and vine-choked swampland. They are one of the many predators of Kenshi who will happily eat their prey while it is still alive, which they do by incapacitating organic beings and sucking them dry (draining the victim's hunger bar).

Blood Spiders are the true "glass cannons" of Kenshi. They are individually quite fragile, but they attack in groups, hit like a truck, and can walk along the bottom of bodies of water. Particularly in The Swamp, this means that being caught between Blood Spiders and a large lake can be a death sentence, as the spiders will simply follow a swimmer into the water and continue attacking them, unlike most pursuers. If faced on open terrain, however, it is relatively easy to outrun a pack. They are also quite weak to bleeding damage but can recover lost blood quickly. If you manage to down one, quickly looting its teeth to kill it is a good idea.

As if all this wasn't bad enough, there is also a stronger, larger variant known as the Elder Blood Spider found in one location.

The Unique Recruit Miu hates spiders passionately and will often let loose with a string of expletives when they are nearby, potentially providing a valuable warning to a player who has not noticed them yet.

## Bonedog
type = Nesting, Roaming

Bonedogs are canine-like creatures which roam the wastes in large packs. They have dark fur and a bony ridge or stripe along each side, with the bony face typical of many Kenshi animals. They are territorial and will aggress when other creatures roam close to their patrols or nests. Some of these beasts, however wild by nature, have been domesticated by people as trusty companions and loyal guardians. They are the favored protectors of Rebel Farmers, among others.

Wild Bonedogs are drawn to the scent of dead bodies and will devour any they find. They will not attempt to devour living characters, even if they are playing dead. If a severed limb is found a Bonedog may "Play 'Limb,'" a playful action where they run around carrying the limb as if it were a stick or toy. They will stop if given a specific Move Order, but once they are done with the Move Order, they will doggedly go back to playing. Other Bonedogs will playfully chase the limb carrier with the action "Limb Envy".

Domesticated Bonedogs may be purchased from Nomad animal shops and caravans, as well as some farm shops. They are not the sturdiest fighters, especially as pups, and sometimes struggle to keep pace with a high-athletics squad, but they are fast-attacking melee combatants with a powerful bite, and will eat absolutely anything, including their beloved severed limbs.
If you have a Bonedog companion, keep in mind that units will not eat out of a bag until they reach 225 hunger. They will eat any food on the ground at 240 hunger as well as grab any Severed Limbs that are laying on the ground at 275 hunger to play with until they get bored shortly after and decide to eat it. The speed at which they get bored and decide to eat the limb is based off their age and hunger. If below 270 hunger and not currently fed (Hunger shown as Variant 1

## Crab
Crabs are large, reddish, crustacean-like creatures that are usually hostile, perhaps because they are crabby. They are strong fighters but quite slow. They mostly roam coastal regions in the east/southeast parts of the continent, including the Howler Maze, The Pits East, and Greenbeach. Their diet consists of fish, fungi, and algae.

Crabs can also be found in Crab Nests which can contain anywhere between 5 to 30 baby crabs. Crabs spawn in a few buildings in the Howler Maze, making exploring those buildings hazardous. Potentially up to 49 Crab Eggs can be found sitting on the ground in a Crab Nest. It is not possible to hatch a crab egg, so these are merely for selling.

There are several unique powerful crabs including the Megacrab, found in the Lost Town. The rest can be met during a Crab Tournament.

Crabs can be purchased from Crab Raiders, who revere the creatures, at Crab Shops found in Crab Town and Crab Village. An adult crab costs approximately c.14,000.

## Domesticated Bull
faction = The Holy NationDomesticated Bulls are tamed animals that can be used for transporting goods over long distances in the large bags which they can carry on their backs. There are other types of Bulls, including Wild Bulls which can be found in roaming groups, and Pack Bulls which can be found in Trade Caravans. The primary difference of Domesticated Bulls is their stubby horns, apparently cut off at a young age.

Domesticated Bulls can be purchased from Holy Farm Leaders found at any Holy Farm. Unlike in the real world, both males and females are referred to as "bulls."

Domesticated Bulls have a 1.2 strength multiplier, but their cut horns mean they deal somewhat less damage overall, with a higher proportion of it being blunt. Additionally, they have less health on their head and heal more slowly when compared to the other bull types. They are clearly specialized by the Holy Nation as beasts of burden rather than battle, with their strength bonus and pathetic damage scaling. They still retain the signature bull charge attack so they can still hold their own against groups of weak opponents.

image1 = Beak Elder.jpgElder Beak Things are, due to their age, much larger, stronger, and faster Beak Things. They are fairly rare and only found in the Bonefields and High Bonefields. They are not a distinct species of Gutters, but rather the same species that have grown to an exceptional size.

Caution is advised when approaching, as it is nearly impossible to escape from one using speed alone. Elder Beak Things' attack radius is bigger than what you might expect as animals weapon reach is increased with age. It makes them capable of hitting large groups of characters if they're too close to each other. Combined with their age-boosted stats, this makes them a terrifyingly powerful enemy.

## Garru
caption1 = This Garru has been heavily injured in battle.
in-game dialogue

Garru are extremely common wild herbivorous animals. They are somewhat comically shaped, with floppy ears, a hollow tail, a camel-like hump, wrinkly skin, and a prominent underbite. Garru often travel in herds with their young, and can be seen roaming over deserts, mountains, and wastes all over the continent. Garru are often tamed as Pack Beasts by the Traders Guild and Western Hive.

Although generally not aggressive, wild Garru can be skittish and may attack if the player or NPCs move around while very close to the herd. A party member with the Smart personality may comment, accurately, that standing still will prevent them from attacking. Simply keeping your distance is also effective. If a party member has the Maniacal or Dumb personality, they may taunt the Garru leading to a fight.  

An enraged Garru is a formidable and sturdy fighter and should not be underestimated. They can frequently be found destroying whole squads of hungry bandits tempted by their plentiful meat and occasionally triumph even over small groups of Beak Things. They have a large health pool and, like all animals, cannot be staggered, making them anything but a soft target.

## Goat
image1 = Beloved Goat.png
Goats are small Herbivores that roam free in many parts of the world, usually in groups of around 8, and can also be found domesticated, mostly by Nomads. They look much like real-world goats except for their prominent bony faceplates. They can be hunted for their Raw Meat and Animal Skin drops, but make tougher melee opponents than their low stats might suggest. A new character who mistakes them for an easy meal will likely get an unpleasant surprise.

Goats do not have Nests, although domesticated ones can be found in Nomad Goat Herder Camps. They can be purchased from those herders, and also from Nomad animal shops and caravans. A unique goat named Cornelius can be purchased in Settled Nomad Village. Like all tamed animals in Kenshi (and many real goats), domesticated goats will happily eat any type of food at all.

Goats are a niche addition to a player squad. They cannot be used to haul goods and are not as sturdy or strong as a Bonedog. But they are relatively fast, not very expensive, mature quickly, and do not eat as much as most other tamed animals, potentially making them useful as expendable scouts/kiters or a low-maintenance choice for early base defense.

20 to 30 hostile Adult Goats can be found in the Tower of Goats in Sinkuun.

## Leviathan
faction=WildlifeA Leviathan is a humongous creature found chiefly in the Leviathan Coast region in the northwest. A towering wild beast with incredible endurance and strong area attacks, it is the largest living creature in Kenshi and can crush an unprepared squad beneath its enormous feet.

Leviathans are sometimes hunted for the expensive pearls they drop, but the sheer difficulty of felling such a massive beast makes even such a pricey reward often not commensurate with the effort. Players more often hunt them to train statistics, especially with crossbows as this can be done in relative safety due to the lumbering beasts' slow movement speed. In-game, the areas frequented by Leviathans are mostly deserted of NPCs and it is rare to find anyone challenging their mighty dominance except the odd suicidal Beak Thing. A Holy Paladin may warn the player to avoid the northwest coast because of the "demons" there, "a hundred times the size of a man".

Leviathans may travel as lone individuals or in small family squads with their young. As one might expect, leviathans will protect their family if the squad is attacked. But attacking a lone leviathan will not aggro those in other roaming squads.

Loners can become territorial if they see a character who is in a squad of three or more, in which case the Leviathan will "GGGRRRROOOOOOO" then attack anyone in that squad. This can occur even if only one squad member is present. The chance for this to occur is 10% for squads of 3, 30% for squads of 4 or more and finally a 50% for squads of 6 or more.

## Skimmer
type = roaming, nesting

Skimmers are large, hostile insectoids native to desert and arid climates. They resemble four-legged antlions with tall antennae and a fanged mouth which they use to feed on corpses.

They spawn primarily in Skimsands, as well as in the adjacent regions of The Eye, Heng, The Great Desert, Bast, with likely migrations to Sinkuun in the latter 2 cases. They are highly aggressive, making them a hazard for new characters starting in the United Cities.

Samurai Gate Guards can frequently be found battling the large pests, usually dispatching them with ease using their polearms. As Skimmers are generally slower than a player character, kiting them to gate guards can be an effective way to get food and cats in the very early game.

Skimmers can spawn in Skimmer Nests and Small Skimmer Nests in Heng, Skimsands, and The Great Desert. While sleeping in a nest, Skimmers are buried in sand and hard to see, and may ambush an unwary passer-by. They can be detected by carefully looking for their white faceplates poking through the sand.

If one Skimmer spawns alone it will hunt without resting. However, Skimmers from nests and larger squads will conceal themselves under the ground to sleep from 6:00 to 23:00, only waking up before then if something walks too close. Once awoken (during the day) Skimmers will go back to sleep after wandering for around 140 in-game minutes, provided they are not in combat.

Bodyparts of adults have around 200-250 HP. Their attacks damage a single target.

## Skin Spider
type = RoamingSkin Spiders are hostile carnivores found primarily in Arach, their neighboring Shun as well as the aptly named Spider Plains, with likely migrations to the Stenn Desert in the latter case.

They also have a significant presence in southeast section of the world, sprawling throughout Bonefields and High Bonefields.

They are large tan insectoid or arachnoid creatures with long, multi-jointed legs and a tiny, creepy head. They hunt anything nearby with high cutting damage attacks from their pointy legs, and they share their smaller cousins' ability to "eat" their prey alive by draining its hunger bar.

Although larger and tougher than Blood Spiders, Skin Spiders are generally less dangerous because they favor open terrain where they are easier to see coming and can fairly easily be outpaced. Like their cousins, they have high bleeding resistance and will wake up quickly should they be knocked out, so it is wise to loot their claws to kill them once downed.

Skin Spiders can be found in enormous swarms of 20-30 in Arach and are rumored to be controlled by that zone's infamous humanoid resident. Anyone hoping to claim the Bugmaster's treasure hoard will need to fight (or sneak) through many waves of these guardian spiders.

Sharp polearms offer a 25–50% damage bonus against animals and can thus be useful when fighting Skin Spiders. The Desert Sabre also provides a 100% damage bonus against Skin Spiders.

Instead of consuming the limbs of a downed individual like a Gutter, the Skin Spider will rapidly drain the prey's hunger, leading to a Strength penalty. This may hinder the victim's movement speed, making it more difficult to escape.
