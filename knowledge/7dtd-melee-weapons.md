## Robotic Sledge
1.  Description
The ' is the simplest form of a turret that can be used automatically or manually for defense. The  is only active when the owner moves within range of the turret.

The perk Robotics Inventor, one of the Intellect skills, can be upgraded to improve the use of the Robotic Turret.

    1.  Crafting

    1.  Manual Mode
The Robotic Sledge can be used manually like other weapons by firing with the left mouse button.

    1.  Sentry Mode
Placing the turret for automatic defense mode is accomplished by right-clicking to activate placement mode. Once the user has decided the placement location and angle, right-clicking again will place the turret. The turret will turn green when placement is available, or red if it cannot be placed in that location. Picking up the turret back up is done with the "e" key. The turret will only actively defend while the user is nearby, without the owning player nearby it will temporarily turn off until the player returns. Typically, only one turret can be actively defending for a given player.  If turrets are placed far enough apart from each other, they will switch activity based on which one the user is closer to, regardless of which one has targets.

Assuming the player is within range of both turrets, if one runs out of ammunition the other will then activate.

Achieving level 5 of the perk Robotics Inventor will allow a player to have two active turrets at a time. Optimal use would include one Robotic Turret and one Robotic Sledge, as one provides decent damage and crowd control while the other provides area denial and decent knockdown time.

## Stun Baton
1.  Description
The Stun Baton is a melee weapon affected by Intellect and the Electrocutioner perk.

The baton gains "charges" on each hit, 1 for normal attacks and 2 for power attacks.  Upon reaching 4 or more charges the next hit will inflict a shock effect on enemies.

While the enemy is being shocked, they will be unable to move or attack and will take 3 damage every second.

Each rank of Electrocutioner increases the duration of the shock effect and the weapon's base damage.  It will not increase the damage per tick of the shock effect, but a longer shock will result in it dealing more total damage.

It may be repaired using Repair Kits.

    1.  Strategy
The Stun baton is a surprisingly deadly weapon, able to hold its own even at high Game Stage values where other melee weapons begin to struggle.  Even zombie bears are no threat as long as you have a charged baton ready for them.

The most effective application of this weapon is to spread out the shocks on as many enemies as possible.  This both takes them out of the fight for a substantial amount of time and allows the damage over time effect of the shock to run its full course, while you deal with other threats.  Aiming for the most dangerous enemies first, such as feral or radiated zombies, is ideal.

The Tech Junkie Volume 6 book drastically improves the rate you can build charges with a stun baton.  It adds a 25% chance (50% on power attacks) to add 4 additional charges which will instantly charge the baton.

With this book and the maximum rank of Electrocutioner you can apply a shock effect to an incredible quantity of enemies by alternating between power attacks to achieve a max charge, and normal attacks to apply it.  Care must be taken to not overextend and run out of stamina while doing this however.

The Nerd Tats candy bought from vending machines will, for 5 minutes, cause Stun Batons to also shock all nearby (1.2m radius) enemies, which can be a powerful buff to apply before a fight with many enemies such as horde night or when doing a Tier 5 mission.

The Stun Baton Repulsor Mod, adds an additional ragdoll effect when a shock is applied which causes the enemy to be flung backwards a short distance. Nerd Tats disables this effect.

They will be able to stand back up during the shock effect, but will be unable to do anything else until the shock ends.

Apart from repositioning dangerous enemies, other applications can include flinging enemies off high places or into hazards such as Wood Spikes or Blade Traps.  Simply ragdolling an enemy may be enough to cause them to fall off angled or rounded surfaces, such as ramps or pipes.

However constantly flinging enemies around, while amusing, can make getting consistent head-shots difficult and may limit your damage output against a single enemy.
