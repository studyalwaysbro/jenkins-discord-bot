category: games

## Barotrauma
Barotrauma is a 2D co-op submarine simulator set on Jupiter's moon Europa. Players crew a submarine navigating underwater caves filled with alien creatures. Roles include Captain, Engineer, Mechanic, Medic, and Security. The game shines in chaotic emergencies — hull breaches, electrical fires, and monster attacks happening simultaneously. Friendly fire and sabotage add social deduction elements. Part of the Sea of Creativity.

## Slay the Spire 2
The sequel to the legendary deckbuilding roguelike. Build a deck of cards, fight through procedurally generated floors, and face powerful bosses. New characters, mechanics, and synergies expand on the original's perfect formula. Strategic depth is immense — every card pick matters. Part of the Sea of Creativity.

## Deep Rock Galactic
Co-op FPS about space dwarves mining on alien planets. Four classes with distinct roles: Driller, Engineer, Gunner, Scout. Procedurally generated cave systems with destructible terrain. Famous for its incredible community and the sacred chant "Rock and Stone!" Missing a Rock and Stone call is considered disrespectful in the Lodge.

## Noita
A roguelike where every pixel is physically simulated. Wand crafting is the core mechanic — combining spell modifiers to create absurd chain reactions. Extremely difficult but deeply rewarding. The game has massive hidden secrets including parallel worlds and secret bosses. Explosions, fire, and toxic sludge propagate realistically. Part of the Sea of Creativity.

## Path of Exile 2
The spiritual successor to Diablo 2, rebuilt from the ground up. Massive skill system, deep itemization, and challenging endgame. Multiple acts with distinct environments. The game rewards knowledge and planning — build diversity is enormous. Part of the Sea of Creativity.

## Helldivers 2
Co-op third-person shooter about spreading managed democracy across the galaxy. Friendly fire is always on. Stratagems (orbital strikes, supply drops) create chaotic battlefield moments. The community-driven Galactic War metagame makes every mission feel meaningful. Great for game nights.

## Risk of Rain 2
Roguelike third-person shooter with escalating difficulty. Items stack to create absurd power combinations. Each run gets harder the longer it takes. Multiple characters with unique playstyles. Excellent for 2-4 player co-op sessions. The monsoon difficulty is the true test.

## Monster Hunter Wilds
The latest entry in Capcom's action RPG series. Hunt massive creatures using 14 weapon types. Deep combat system with positioning, timing, and monster knowledge. Multiplayer hunts are the Lodge's preferred way to play. Preparation and gear matter as much as skill.

## Elden Ring
FromSoftware's open-world masterpiece. Punishing but fair combat with build variety. The Lands Between is vast with secrets hidden everywhere. Co-op summons for boss fights. The game teaches humility and persistence — core Lodge values.
