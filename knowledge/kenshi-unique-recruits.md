## Skeleton Survivor
We do what we must to survive... But nowhere is this more apparent than the swamplands. Tread carefully, outlander, this is a town where the strong step on the weak to survive. If you need my protection, you can have it for 2500 Cats.

Skeleton Survivor is a randomly named Unique Recruit. They are a skeleton who can "distinguish the underdogs from the survivors". They can be hired for 2,500 Cats.

They can be found in Shark, no matter which faction is in control of The Swamps.

They have the "Player SKELETON" Dialogue Package.

Stats Randomize = 3
