## Kenshi — The Pillar of Suffering
Kenshi is an open-world squad-based RPG set in a brutal post-apocalyptic desert. There is no chosen one. You start as nobody — a starving wanderer in a world that wants you dead. Characters can lose limbs, be enslaved, left for dead, eaten by wildlife. The game has no hand-holding, no quest markers, no leveling through combat alone. You must train your skills by getting beaten down and surviving. Base building, squad management, crafting, and trading are all viable paths. The world is divided into hostile factions including the Holy Nation (religious zealots), United Cities (corrupt slavers), and Shek Kingdom (warrior culture). Kenshi teaches that meaning comes from struggle, not from being special. It is the first pillar of the Holy Trinity.

## Kenshi Tips and Strategy
Start by mining copper near a town to earn money. Hire recruits from bars. Train athletics by running constantly. Build a base only after your squad can defend it. Avoid the Holy Nation if you have non-human party members. Research is critical — tech unlocks better gear. Martial arts becomes overpowered late game. Limb replacement with robotic prosthetics makes characters stronger than before. The Fog Islands are extremely dangerous early but have valuable loot. Always carry a sleeping bag. Hunger management is the biggest early challenge.

## Caves of Qud — The Pillar of Mutation
Caves of Qud is a science-fantasy roguelike set in a far-future jungle. The world is layered with ancient technology, mutated organisms, and deep lore. Players choose between True Kin (pure human with cybernetics) or Mutated Human (random mutations that define your build). Mutations range from growing extra arms to shooting fire from your hands to developing precognition. The game features procedurally generated dungeons, a rich dialogue system, factions to join or betray, and deep crafting through tinkering. Death is permanent in classic mode. The water economy is unique — water is currency. Caves of Qud represents the pillar of creativity and adaptation.

## Caves of Qud Tips and Strategy
Mutations to prioritize: Multiple Legs (movement speed), Carapace (armor), Corrosive Gas Generation (crowd control). True Kin get access to cybernetic implants found throughout the world. Always carry trade goods — artifacts and books sell well. Join the Barathrumites faction for access to tinkering recipes. The Rust Wells contain powerful artifacts but are heavily guarded. Cooking with ingredients grants temporary and permanent bonuses. Water ritual with NPCs reveals secrets and grants faction reputation. Domination (psychic mutation) lets you control any creature temporarily.

## Battle Brothers — The Pillar of Brotherhood
Battle Brothers is a turn-based tactical RPG where you lead a mercenary company through a low-fantasy medieval world. Every brother is procedurally generated with unique backgrounds, traits, and stats. Death is permanent and devastating — you will lose brothers you've invested dozens of hours into. Combat is hex-based with positioning, morale, fatigue, and injury systems creating emergent drama. The game has three crises (Greenskin Invasion, Undead Scourge, Holy War) that reshape the world map. No magic, no chosen one — just steel, blood, and the bonds between your men. Battle Brothers represents the pillar of brotherhood and sacrifice.

## Battle Brothers Tips and Strategy
Early game: take easy contracts, avoid camps with named enemies. Hire cheap backgrounds (farmhands, brawlers) and look for hidden stars (high stat growth). Invest in a strong frontline with shields first. Crossbows are better than bows early game. The Sergeant (rally skill) is essential — morale breaks lose battles. Nimble vs Battle Forged is the key defensive choice for late game. Nimble works with light armor and high initiative; Battle Forged works with heavy armor. Famed weapons from champion enemies are game-changers. Named enemies always drop loot. The Kraken, Ijirok, and Black Monolith are the hardest legendary fights.
