## Factions
In Kenshi, there are several different factions you can join and fight against. Some factions will be hostile to any person they see by default, such as: Cannibals, Berserkers and Swamp Ninjas.

You can potentially achieve perks and various bonuses by getting in the good graces of certain factions (such as handing in common bandit bounties).

While many factions have rivals, it should be noted that an alliance with one signals cooperation with the faction in question, not hostility towards their rivals. Unless your alliance involves actions or pledges that explicitly antagonizes said faction's rivals, your relationship with said faction's rivals remains unchanged/neutral, as support for one group doesn’t inherently translate to aggression towards the rest, although your relations would likely sour as a result.

A faction would be considered "Allied" if their relation towards you is +50 and above, and "Enemy" if -30 and below.

By attacking a faction, you will damage your relations with them and may eventually become an enemy. Some factions have special methods to repair relations (such as Pacifiers), but most do not. So, pick your battles wisely.

The United Cities are closely allied with many factions such as Bounty Hunters, Slave Traders, and the Traders Guild. The Empire values wealth above all else. They are opposed by the destitutes of their region, the Rebel Farmers. Its territory is populated by Empire Peasants. They are also at conflict with The Holy Nation.

    1.  Western Hive
This is a faction of the insect-like Hive race. The Western Hive proper is located on an island off the western coast, but a majority of the Hive lives on the continent, in series of Hive Villages stretched along the river in Vain, as well as the neighboring region Dreg. A few other, however, live rather isolated from their main civilization at Sinkuun.

Lost Drones and much of the Empire Peasants originated from this faction.

## Pacifiers
Pacifiers are characters found in various locations that can repair relations with their parent faction if the player has gotten on their bad side, for a price. They have a variable fee, demanding more for their services the worse the player's relations are, ranging from c.15,000 to c.100,000. Some particularly bad instances may require multiple payments, as they have a limit to how much they can fix at a time.

There are also sometimes restrictions in place that will prevent the player from fixing their relations with certain factions. These generally involve things like being allied with their enemies, or the killing/imprisonment of important characters from their faction. The Pacifier will explain why he is refusing to provide service to the player if he does though, providing a clue as to what might be done to rectify the situation.

## Broken Ones
Error Code 0xFFFFFF
The Broken Ones are a Faction of broken Soldierbots. They can be found in the Reprogramming Workshop located in the north-east section of the Deadlands. Currently, this faction can only be found in this workshop.

Initially, some players may assume these characters to be hostile, since most Broken Skeletons are. However, if a player character enters this workshop, all skeletons will automatically become allies with that character. This results in the skeletons to have the 'Following' goal indefinitely for that character.

Since they are allies, the skeletons will attempt to protect the player character during combat. However, these skeletons fail to differentiate whether foreign squads are hostile towards the player and will attack anyone they encounter, including non-player allies and shopkeepers. If attacked by the player, they will not initiate combat and will attempt to flee.

Despite having some basic dialogue and the option to chat, the player cannot interact with this faction and any attempt at communication will result in an "Error." response.

It is possible for them to be lured out of their building by iron spiders and other enemies and in this case some of them may die.

## Cannibals
hostile_towards = everyone
Deadcat Vendor

The Cannibals (AKA the "Painted Tribe") are a group of tribal humanoids native to the Cannibal Plains, Darkfinger and other surrounding zones. While most densely present in the northern areas of Kenshi, these groups are known to migrate and can expand as far away as The Great Desert, Bast, Okran's Valley and Dreg.

Cannibals will attack other factions and carry unconscious organic members of other factions to their camp or Village. When the cages start to get full, they will periodically start beating their prisoners to death and throwing them into the cooking pits.

They will not eat Skeletons or animals, but they are still hostile to them. Strangely, similar to Skeletons and Hivers, all Cannibals are immune to Acid.

    1.  History

According to the Machinists' book, the The Cannibal Plains: De-evolution Of Man?, the "Painted Tribe" has existed for thousands of years, and they are suspected to actually be a race of "devolved" humans.

One theory suggests that the Cannibals are descended from inhabitants of the northwestern Ancient Labs, and started degenerating due to environmental toxins, the effects of lab experiments, or simply severe famine.

Another theory suggests that the Cannibals existed in the same time frame as the Ancient Lab inhabitants, with the latter being killed off by migrating Cannibals.

The Cannibals are known to feed on one another as well, since most outsiders avoid the Cannibal Plains.

According to a forum post by a Lofi-Fi developer, the Deadcats were described as "Rebel Descendants", though it is unclear who they are rebels of.

Judging by their name, one possible theory is that they were the descendants of humans who rebelled and broke away from the Second Empire and their leader Cat-Lon.

There are also a few dialogue options that allow the player character to simply stab the War Leader in the face (or guts) mid-conversation. This will cause him to scream in pain, and his entire army will flee. However, they will show back up a few days later, doubled in size.

## Fogmen
caption1="Lost their minds to parasite. Controls them from the inside."

The Fogmen (also known as the Deadhive) are a savage group of Hivers lurking within the Fog Islands. Anyone who enters the Fog Islands are doomed to be attacked by the Fogmen, who are a constant threat to the people in the nearby town of Mongrel.

    1.  Overview
Upon attacking and incapacitating any non-skeleton characters, nearby Fogmen will attempt to carry the injured person(s) back to their camps, where they will be tied to a pole and eaten alive by Fog Princes, or if no Princes are nearby, Fog Heavies engage in a ritualistic feast. It should be noted that even if all nearby Fogmen are killed, the injured person is still in immense danger as new Fogmen can always spawn in to take their place. They will often not attempt to fight after kidnapping their victim, merely running in to get the body and retreating back to camp. While it is possible to rescue captives, as their limbs will be eaten first, heavy caution is advised.

Their main weakness is that Fogmen are only barely able warriors and are individually very fragile. To make up for this weakness, they roam in large groups of Fogmen and Fog Heavies. When fighting a group of Fogmen it's not uncommon that others may take notice and join in as well, making it extremely easy to find yourself surrounded. With their vast manpower, they can eventually take down even a skilled warrior.

They are known to supposedly be controlled by a neurological parasite, but the details are unknown, and the reliability of these rumors are questionable. It appears that it is impossible for them to regain control of themselves by any means. Whatever causes their behavior, they are completely feral beyond their ritual cannibalization of humanoids. They have no voice lines beyond incoherent screaming.

If both of Hiver Queens are dead, Fogmen will spread to all of the former Hiver locations, supporting a theory that Hivers cannot function without pheromones of their queens, even if separated from them.

    1.  Deathyards
The Fog Islands are littered with so-called Deathyards, which are large camps of Fogmen that spawn groups of Fogmen. Within the center of these Fogmen nest are prison pole from which people captured by the Fogmen are eaten alive. Typically, Deathyards are spawned within one of the many large craters of the fog islands. Fogmen regularly patrol the roads which crisscross the Fog Islands, and it is possible to easily be overwhelmed by a horde of Fogmen due to the presence of several Deathyards located next to each other.

## Nameless
Nameless is the player's controlled faction. Although initially without a name, it can be renamed at any time by going to the "Faction" menu and clicking on "Faction Name". The Nameless is a completely player-based faction. There are no preset squads, faction relations, or gear belonging to it unless the player's choice of Game Start determines so.

Player's faction name appears on weaponry and armor crafted by a playable character, for example "This Horse Chopper was crafted by Red of Nameless". With the use of a Plastic Surgeon and faction renaming, this feature can be used to create short "notes" within a game. For example, it is possible to create "Uniform of Archers" tag on an armor item by using this technique. In this case "Uniform" is the temporary name of the player's faction and "Archers" is a renamed character, author of the item. ## Shek Kingdom
image1=shek_logo.png
Shek Barman Admag

Homeland of the Shek and headed by queen Esata "the Stone Golem", the Shek Kingdom is a very sparsely populated and tribalistic faction that resides almost entirely within the ranges of Stenn Desert, centered in their capital, Admag.

They are a nation composed of battlehungry and stubborn tribal raiders who were unified under Kral, a legendary Shek warrior who consolidated the warring Shek tribes into a centralized nation hundreds of years ago, and have raided and warred against their neighbors ever since.

Their entire culture revolves around the teachings of Kral, which emphasizes sacrificing their own life for the prospect of glory. This pursuit of glory almost led to the destruction of the Kingdom under the reign of Shager, who had extensively depopulated his own kingdom in total war against The Holy Nation in futile assaults that were defeated due to being far outnumbered in the field.

Because of these strong beliefs in ‘honor through death in battle’ etc, the Shek make a formidable enemy, persistent and fearless in combat. But their warrior attitude also disadvantages the Shek, as their suicidal mindset means that their numbers are diminishing and weakening them as a nation.

They aim to assert their supremacy above all other nations and races (i.e. Humans and Hivers) that they consider to be weaker. In pursuit of this ideal, they have taken an aggressive stance with regards to the outside nations, and they are no strangers to warring, raiding, killing and demanding tribute from their neighbours to assert their dominance.

However, their recent assaults and total wars against The Holy Nation (and United Cities) has crippled their population demographics and driven their country to the brink of extinction, and they were forced to relent on their aggressive policies or face certain early destruction.

As a result, they had to sign a truce with UC, and also forbade the attacking of outsiders in their cities to focus their efforts on plaguing The Holy Nation. They also opened up their cities to outside trade until they manage to recover themselves to a sufficient degree. Their society has suffered a great fracture in cohesion, and a significant portion of of their Sheks have already deserted the main kingdom itself to join Kral's Chosen under Flying Bull, with the rest on the smaller ones like Berserkers and Band of Bones.

They love to hate Okranites, and will often start unprovoked assaults in an attempt to kill any that they see. Their nation often conducts raids to terrorize their nation.  The goal of their leadership revolves entirely around weakening The Holy Nation's military command to gain foothold in their territory.

Fighting prowess are valued above all else in their society, to such a degree that they have sidelined all intellectual pursuits, and vast majority of Sheks are illiterate as a result.

## Starving Bandits
Starvers attacking

The Starving Bandits are a frequently seen hostile faction found roaming primarily in and around the wilds and peripheries of Holy Nation lands, they are similar to the Starving Vagrants that are found in United Cities lands but are somewhat more organized since they are always found in groups (sometimes gigantic ones) and have leaders.

They are made up of countless nobodies, destitutes, thugs and beggars who, instead of working or begging inside settlements, have ganged up and resorted to banditry out in the wilds to feed themselves instead.  They tend to roam around zones in gangs of 8, 13 or 42 and look for any wanderers (usually the player) to prey upon. Due to their fragile states, many are weak and untrained for combat, but they have strength in numbers.

## The Holy Nation
trade_culture = The Holy Nation Trade Culture

The Holy Nation (sometimes also referred to as Holy Empire) is a very large theocratic Greenlander state ruled by a Phoenix whom they believe to be a reincarnation of their first ruler, and is rumored to be over 5000 years old. Their denizens are concentrated mostly in and around Okran's Pride but could be found on over a quarter of the entire continent. They are the largest faction in terms of population, outnumbering others by several dozen times.

They are a zealous farming society whose religion encompasses all aspects of their life. Their religion, Okranism, is based around the dualism of the Okran, Lord Of Light, and Narko, Demoness of Darkness. Holy Nation citizens are often interchangeably referred to as Okranites. They live their life based on the doctrines set forth by the first Phoenix, which elevates men as the virtuous role model that assumes the role of stewardship over women.

Prior to the game start they were a nation constantly plagued by Sheks who have been raiding and warring against them for centuries, this all changed under the rising of their 62nd Phoenix, a harsh and ambitious leader who saw the futility of merely defending against the Shek raids. Recognizing that peace would never come from a passive posture alone, he launched a bold and decisive military campaign - The Holy Crusade - an initiative that sought to rid the Kingdom's raids once and for all.

The Okranites - bolstered by the initiative of their leader - fought tooth and nail against the raiders of Shek Kingdom whose leader Shager launched all-out offensives of their own in response. In a war of attrition with heavy casualties on both sides, the forces of HN prevailed as a result of their overwhelming numbers and struck a decisive blow to the Shek's leadership, decimating their forces and nearly driving them into extinction.

At the final phases of the conflict, they have managed to secure their borders as the Shek no longer had the strength to raid their nation effectively. But the relative peace is a fragile one, as the few remaining warriors were preserved by their new leadership bide their time and raid at the edges of their territory, and their threat isn't entirely gone.

On another front, in recent times after Tengu came into power, simmering tensions between them and United Cities have broken out into open conflict at the border of Okran's Valley - Bast. The forces of HN have successfully overwhelmed and pushed UC back to Bast in a single day and razed the entire region in the process, the borders have since become conflict zones with constant skirmishes between the 2 giants, where HN has been fairly content simply keeping UC at bay.

## United Cities
The United Cities, A United Power
The United Cities (A.K.A. the "Empire" or  the "Desert Empire") is an expansionist, feudal slaver society. It is the largest faction by land area and has towns of drastically varying population sizes dispersed throughout the continent, including The Great Desert, Heng, The Hook, Bonefields, and the Stormgap Coast.

Its northern population is heavily concentrated on the towns of Stoat, Heng, and Heft, while its southern population is concentrated on Drifter's Last.

Ruled by Tengu and his inner circle, the Empire is plagued with endemic corruption, and monetary worth is the prime measure of a person in Empire culture. Those suffering extreme poverty are considered to be potentially dangerous criminal elements and are often enslaved as a result.

Slavery and serfdom are important pillars of the Empire, filled up by the bottom of Empire society such as the vagrants and other types of bandits, providing food and material. Another pillar is the Traders Guild. The guild brings their cities great wealth and prosperity. The Empire possesses advanced technology, high-quality equipment, and valuable luxuries imported from distant lands. It considers anti-slavery ideology to be a form of terrorism.

The UC is engaged in an ongoing conflict with The Holy Nation, with the heaviest fighting taking place at the border of Okran's Valley - Bast. The UC suffered a great defeat at Bast where they were pushed back by HN forces and lost the hold on the region in a single day. Since the razing of Bast, the conflict has frozen, turning into a stalemate.

The drug Hashish is highly illegal, and the samurai guards are obsessed with purging it from their streets to such an extent that even the material used to produce it, Hemp, is also outlawed.

The United Cities does not tolerate Hemp and Hemp-related goods and production facilities. Owning any building such as Biofuel Distillery (Hemp), Hemp Farm, Hemp Processor or a Hydroponic Hemp will result in a Drug Bust raid against the player if this building is discovered by the authorities. It is likewise illegal to possess or import Bloodrum, Hashish or Hemp into the Empire, despite evidence of use and possession of said substances by the privileged Nobles. "Rules for thee, not for me" is very much what the Empire stands on.

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a colour scheme attached.

    1.  History
History of Kenshi puts the founding of United Cities and Traders Guild between 100 ~ 1000 years ago, sometime after Shek Kingdom was founded, and is rumored to come from the ashes of a former empire such as the Second Empire.

## Yabuta Outlaws
Yabuta Outlaws is a minor Hostile Faction. The Yabuta Outlaws are created after the character Yabuta of the Sands is released from Tengu's Vault. Despite ostensibly created under the leadership of Yabuta, he is nowhere to be found in this faction.

Members of this faction can colour armour and clothing due to a colour scheme attached.
