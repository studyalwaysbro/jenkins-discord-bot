## Akabeko
flavor = "Muuu~"The Akabeko is a common relic that makes the first attack played each combat deal an additional 8 damage.
    1.  Strategy
Akabeko is essentially a hefty 1-attack Strength boost. This relic benefits multihit cards, such as Pummel and Ragnarok, as the additional damage is applied to each attack done by the card.

    1.  Synergy

- Bag of Marbles: If your first attack played is on turn 1, it will benefit from the Vulnerable given by Bag of Marbles.
- Bag of Preparation, Ring of the Snake, and Ring of the Serpent: The additional card draw means it is more likely for an attack that greatly benefits from Akabeko to be drawn on turn 1.
- Bottled Flame: Bottling a multihit attack such as Riddle with Holes allows a guaranteed powerful turn 1 attack.
- Wreath of Flame can stack its effect with Akabeko, allowing for stronger multi-hit attacks.
- Dramatic Entrance is innate, costs 0, and gains another 8 damage applied to all enemies.
- Wrath and Divinity Stances: The additional damage given by Akabeko is doubled / tripled by these Stances.
- Gambling Chip and Gambler's Brew: Allows the player to mulligan cards that do not benefit from Akabeko.
- Reaper: The additional damage from Akabeko counts towards the healing given by Reaper.
- Louses are much more likely to be killed instead of Curling Up after being attacked.


- Ordinary attacks such as Flash of Steel may not be played if the player decides to wait for a stronger card like Riddle with Holes.


- Innate Non-attack cards, Bottled Tornado, Bottled Lightning: Innate cards take up 1 of your 5 default draws, therefore making it harder to draw an attack that benefits from Akabeko.
- Nemesis: Can completely negate Akabeko's effect with its Intangible.
- Being Weakened can reduce Akabeko's effect.

    1.  Trivia
- An Akabeko is a traditional toy from the Aizu region of Japan.
  - Aizu legend claims that the toy is based on a real cow that was used to build the Enzō-ji Temple of Yanaizu in the 9th century.

## Anchor
Anchor is a common relic that provides 10 Block at the start of each combat.
    1.  Strategy
This relic's ability makes your turn 1 easier, as you do not need to spend as much Energy on defense. This means you could either attack right away, or, more effectively, play Power cards on your first turn safely. Because of this, decks that rely on (likely Innate) Powers find this relic very useful.

    1.  Synergy

- Bag of Preparation or Ring of the Snake: The additional 2 cards in your starting hand may be played safely.
- Body Slam: Does 10 additional damage.
- Bottled Flame and Bottled Tornado: You may play the bottled Attack or Power safely without worrying much about taking damage.
- Calipers, Barricade, Blur: The initial 10 Block may now carry into other turns.
- Runic Pyramid, Well-Laid Plans, Equilibrium: Block cards drawn on turn 1 may be Retained for later turns, therefore not making them useless when coupled with Anchor.


- Bottled Lightning: Allows to play the bottled Skill safely, but the Skill may also be a Block card.
- Boot Sequence: Mostly becomes redundant with this relic and takes one space on your first draw. However, together they can be useful against hard-hitters such as Taskmaster mob.


- Orichalcum: Will not work on first turn if any of the initial 10 Block remains at the end of your turn.

## Ancient Tea Set
The Ancient Tea Set is a common relic that provides 2 Energy at the start of the first combat encountered after entering a Rest Site.

This relic does not spawn beyond floor 48, excluding Endless mode.

    1.  Strategy
This relic's ability allows the player to play more cards on turn 1 after entering a Rest Site, which means it is more likely for a combat to end much faster than it normally would. This gives the player an incentive to route towards Rest Sites. Since boss combats are always preceded with a Rest Site, this relic guarantees 2 additional Energy at the start of boss combats (barring some exceptions, see the Secret Portal event and the Ascension 20 modifier).

It is important to note that entering ? rooms or Treasure Rooms does not mean that the effect of the relic is wasted, as the Energy gain only triggers when entering a combat.

    1.  Synergy

- X Energy Cost Cards (Whirlwind, Skewer, Tempest, etc.): The additional Energy makes playing these cards on turn 1 much more viable.
- Innate Powers (Infinite Blades+, Hello World+, etc.): The additional Energy allows these cards to be played without devoting your first turn to them.
- Card Draw (Acrobatics, Shrug It Off, Battle Trance, etc.): The additional Energy gained from Ancient Tea Set allows you to use cards that draw more cards, while still having enough Energy to use the cards drawn.
- Bag of Preparation or Ring of the Snake: The extra card draw in addition to the 2 Energy provided by Ancient Tea Set allows you to have a much more powerful first turn.
- Ice Cream: Allows you to conserve the additional Energy gained from Ancient Tea Set between turns, giving the effect a much wider impact.

## Art of War
The Art of War is a Common Relic that provides an additional Energy next turn if you do not play any attack cards in your current turn.

    1.  Strategy
This relic benefits during turns where you need to use all Energy to block fully in order to negate a large amount of damage. This relic is also useful during turn 1 on characters like the Defect and the Silent, as a good number of decks for the aforementioned characters include a turn 1 where only powers or skills are played.

    1.  Synergy

- Pocketwatch: On turns where Pocketwatch activates, there is a good chance you did not play any attacks. This allows you to benefit more from the additional energy next turn, as you will have more cards to work with due to Pocketwatch.
- Snecko Eye: On turns where Art of War activates, the additional energy will allow you to play more cards if Snecko Eye randomizes cards into mostly high costs.
- Letter Opener: Allows you to deal damage without playing attack.
- The Bomb: The Bomb is a skill which deals damage, meaning you can deal damage without losing the benefit of Art of War.
- Pressure Points does damage without being an attack.
- Poison can be applied with skills, which is damage without being an attack.


- All attack cards: Prevents Art of War from triggering.
- Double Tap: Playing this skill would be a waste of Energy unless you played an attack, therefore preventing Art of War from triggering.
- Ornamental Fan, Nunchaku, Kunai, Shuriken: These relics reliance on attacking clashes directly with Art of War.

    1.  Trivia

The relic references the Art of War, an ancient manuscript on military tactics written by Sun Tzu.

  - Art of War should now show the correct energy icon.

## Bag of Marbles
The Bag of Marbles is a common relic that applies 1 Vulnerable to all enemies at the start of each combat.

    1.  Strategy
The Bag of Marbles naturally benefits decks with a turn 1 that involves dealing damage, by allowing the player to deal even more damage. Even if your turn 1 does not focus on dealing damage, the Vulnerable will help negate some Artifact charges on certain enemies, allowing your subsequent turns to be more powerful.

    1.  Synergy

- Bottled Flame: If a powerful attack is bottled, the Vulnerable will allow that attack to become even more powerful.
- Innate attacks, such as Backstab, Dramatic Entrance and Mind Blast: Innate attacks are able to benefit from Vulnerable and deal more damage.
- Champion Belt: Applies Weak in addition to the Vulnerable. This makes turn 1 a lot safer, as well as speeding up Artifact negation by a large margin.
- Turn 1 Energy relics, such as Lantern and Ancient Tea Set: More Energy allows you to play more attack cards that benefit from Vulnerable.
- Anchor: Protects you for your first turn, allowing you to take advantage of Vulnerable.
- Bag of Preparation, Ring of the Snake and Ring of the Serpent: More card draw allows you to play more attacks.
- Ninja Scroll: Adds free attacks to your hand that benefit from Vulnerable.
- Mutagenic Strength: Makes all of your attacks played on turn 1 stronger.
- Akabeko: increases damage of your first attack, which is also amplified by Vulnerable.

- Bottled Lightning, Bottled Tornado and any Innate powers or skills, such as Infinite Blades+ and Boot Sequence: Innate cards take up some of your draw, therefore you are much less likely to draw attacks that benefit from Vulnerable.
- Gremlin Visage: Greatly lowers the amount of damage you can do on turn 1, thus making the Vulnerable a lot less useful.

## Bag of Preparation
The Bag of Preparation is a common relic that draws an additional 2 cards at the start of each combat. Its effect is identical to the Ring of the Snake, the Silent's default starting relic.

    1.  Strategy
This relic is very powerful despite its rarity. Its effect synergizes with many other relics and cards, and the additional card draw increases the potency of your first turn. However, if you draw too many key cards and are unable to play them all, this relic could possibly set you back for a few turns.

    1.  Interactions

- Cloak Clasp: Gives additional protection at the end of your first turn, which is generally the turn in which you are most vulnerable.
- Spirit Shield: Greatly increases the impact of a Spirit Shield played on the first turn.
- Ring of the Snake, Ring of the Serpent, Snecko Eye: Additional draw from these relics makes your turn 1 very powerful, as you will have 9 cards in your hand (8 with Ring of the Serpent).
- Gambling Chip: You can mulligan additional cards drawn by Bag of Preparation.
- Lantern and Ancient Tea Set: The extra Energy for the first turn along with additional card draw from Bag of Preparation greatly increases the impact of your first turn.
- Mutagenic Strength: More Strength for your first turn in addition to the card draw makes your turn 1 attacks much more potent.


- Ninja Scroll and Holy Water: The generated cards takes priority over Bag of Preparation, and could clog your hand as you cannot have more than 10 cards on your hand at a time, potentially rendering additional draw useless.

    1.  Trivia
- The relic's art shows the same backpack as the card Prepared.

## Blue Candle
Allows playing Curses, similar to what Medical Kit does for statuses but with an extra cost.

Playing a Curse does not cost any Energy.

    1.  Tips

-  "Lose 1 HP" is not damage and will ignore Block. However, it can be reduced by Tungsten Rod.
-  If the Necronomicurse is played, a new one will be added to your hand. While this means you can't escape the Necronomicurse, you can play it as many times as you want, and that interaction can be exploited in a number of ways. It allows you to take arbitrary amounts of damage in one-damage increments, triggering useful on-damage effects like Rupture or Blood for Blood; each play still counts as an exhaust, so it will trigger on-exhaust effects like Dead Branch, Dark Embrace, Charon's Ashes; and each one counts as a card played, so it can trigger things like Panache or A Thousand Cuts.

    1.  Interactions

## Brimstone
class = Ironclad

    1.  Strategy
Brimstone works best if you have a deck that can make the most use of its Strength gain.  Cards like Sword Boomerang, Twin Strike, and Heavy Blade receive the damage boost 2-5 more times than other attacks, and Limit Break can be used to rapidly increase your strength even further. Reaper also works well with Brimstone, as the additional strength allows you to regain more health.

Players who intend to face the Corrupt Heart in Act 4 will want to consider how they can mitigate the enemy strength buff, since that boss has an attack that hits 12(15) times.  Any increase to the Heart's Strength makes this attack far more deadly, so cards like Disarm or Dark Shackles are quality pick ups. As with any build, Intangible and Weak can make the incoming damage more manageable.

## Bronze Scales
Bronze Scales is a common relic that gives your character 3 Thorns at the start of each combat. This means every time an enemy lands a hit on you, blocked or not, they will take 3 damage.

    1.  Strategy

Since it grants Thorns, enemies will take damage every time they land a hit on your character, whether it deals damage or not. This makes this relic especially effective against enemies that rely on multiple-hits attacks such as Book of Stabbing, Byrds, Snake Plant, and Corrupt Heart.

Also, since the enemy's Block fades at the start of their turn, it means that they have no way to prevent Thorns from directly damaging their HP.

    1.  Synergy


- Block: Protects yourself from damage while dealing damage against enemy hits.
- Stacks fully with Caltrops, increases the potency of Thorns even further.
- Complements nicely with Flame Barrier, which itself grants a hefty amount of Block to prevent damage and has an effect similar to Thorns tied to it, which increases the damage against enemy hits even further.
- Reducing enemy Strength or applying Weak allows you to take more hits without receiving much damage. Consider cards like Disarm, Malaise, Piercing Wail, Blind, or Dark Shackles.

    1.  Trivia
- The flavor text says that these scales belong to The Guardian. However, The Guardian's Sharp Hide buff works differently than Thorns, dealing damage to the player per attack card played instead of per hit landed.

## Busted Crown
class = AnyBusted Crown is a Boss Relic that grants 1 extra Energy each turn, but restricts the options available when picking cards.

While the player has this Relic, they will be offered 2 fewer cards on Card Reward screens. Note that this doesn't just apply to post-battle rewards - Tiny House, Sensory Stone, and other opportunities to pick cards will also be reduced.

However, only card rewards that can permanently add a card to the deck are affected by Busted Crown, card select screens of Foreign Influence, Discovery, Toolbox, Nilry's Codex, Attack Potion, Skill Potion, and Power Potion always consists of 3 cards. (these are also not affected by Question Card)

    1.  Strategy
If your deck is at least nearly prepared to win the rest of the run, Busted Crown is effectively a free source of Energy. Early in the game, though, it can represent a crippling disadvantage.

When deciding whether to take the Crown, consider your options for getting more card selection. If you have Dream Catcher or a large amount of money, you may be able to make do. Bear in mind that the Rare boss reward cards will also be reduced!

    1.  Synergies
Question Card adds another choice to card reward screens, leaving you with two options.

Like all Energy buffs, Busted Crown allows you to play expensive cards, cards with draw effects, and X-cost cards with less risk.

    1.  Trivia
This item is likely a reference to the trinket "Cracked Crown" from the Binding of Isaac: Rebirth.

## Relics
Relics are permanent items that provide a passive bonus for the rest of the run. Most relics are available to all characters, with some exceptions (indicated for each relic). The Ironclad and Silent each have access to 11 unique relics while the Defect and Watcher only have 9 apiece.

Starter Relics are granted upon starting a run with the corresponding character.

Common, Uncommon, and Rare Relics can be found from a variety of sources. Usually, the odds of getting a particular rarity of relic is 3:2:1 (50%/33%/17%). Chests have their own rarity chances, which result in the chances of getting each rarity being (49%/42%/9%).

Boss Relics can only be found from Bosses (or in Neow's starting choice, traded for Starter Relic).

Event Relics can only be found from specific event outcomes.

Finally, Shop Relics can only be purchased from the Merchant.

The Ring of the Serpent is a Boss Relic which can only be obtained by the Silent. It replaces the Ring of the Snake and draws 1 additional card at the start of your turn instead of 2 additional cards but only at the start of combat.

On mobile versions, a bug causes it to make you draw two extra cards per turn instead of one.

You must possess Ring of the Snake for it to appear.

Since it replaces Ring of the Snake, you actually lose 1 card draw on turn 1 ( 7 vs 6 cards ), card neutral on turn 2 (12 vs 12), and only starts to be positive on turn 3 (17 vs 18).

If a deck can confidently deal with the loss of early card draw, (Bag of Preparation, Acrobatics, etc.) it may be worth considering to take it so you can better handle longer fights such as bosses. However, other Boss Relics are likely to be of more value while also keeping your Ring of the Snake.

## Runic Pyramid
At the end of your turn, you no longer Discard your hand.

Runic Pyramid is a Boss Relic.

    1.  Tips
-  While keeping your entire hand is extremely powerful, keep the ten-card hand limit in mind — if you're unable to reliably play, discard, or otherwise dispose of five cards a turn, the pyramid will eventually cause you to draw fewer cards.
-  It interacts particularly badly with Wounds, Curses, and other Unplayables, non-Ethereal cards that can linger in your hand forever or even fill it entirely. However, end of turn effects (Burn, Decay, Doubt, Regret, and Shame) will still discard themselves at the end of the turn. Pain and Normality are particularly harmful. For this reason, a Blue Candle can be a particularly useful pairing for dealing with curses (or a Medical Kit for dealing with Wounds).
  -  This drawback can be circumvented with cards that Discard or Exhaust cards from your hand, such as Survivor or True Grit.
-  This relic is particularly powerful with small cycle-based decks, such as Rampage. If you can get your deck down to 10 cards after powers and exhausts are played, you can guarantee you'll see that key card every turn.
-  Despite having a similar effect, this relic does not Retain your hand. It merely stops you from discarding cards at the end of your turn. Cards that synergize with Retain, specifically Establishment, have no additional synergy with Runic Pyramid.

## Snecko Eye
The Snecko Eye is a Boss Relic. You draw an additional 2 cards at the start of each turn, but begin each combat inflicted with the Confused debuff.

    1.  Effect
-  All cards drawn have their costs randomized from 0 to 3 Energy. See the Confused page for an exhaustive list of effects.
-  The player is inflicted with Confused at the start of combat before the Clockwork Souvenir gives themArtifact. As a result, Confused is never blocked by the Clockwork Souvenir.
-  If Confused is removed by the Orange Pellets, newly drawn cards for the rest of the combat will not have their costs randomized. However, any cards that had their costs changed already will keep those costs for the rest of combat.
-  Placing a card into your hand with a card like Seek or Secret Weapon does not count as drawing, and will not randomize the cards' costs. This includes cards created during combat such as Shiv and cards copied with Nightmare or Dual Wield.

    1.  Tips
-  The Snecko Eye's extra 2 cards per turn provides enormous value on its own.
- Each card drawn has a 25% chance of costing 0, increasing the value of effects that draw extra cards.
- Every card's average energy cost is 1.5 with Snecko Eye. Consequently, cards costing 2 or more have better value than they normally do, and cards costing 1 or less have worse value than usual. However, many low-cost cards are still valuable enough to take with Snecko Eye.
- The possibility of drawing 0-cost cards greatly complicates the analysis of how the Snecko Eye impacts your energy. Assuming 3 energy, 7 card draw, and prioritizing playing lower cost cards, the average number of card plays per turn is 3.83.
-  The Snecko Eye works especially well with the Gambling Chip to clear out high-cost or low-value cards on turn 1 and draw to replace them.
- Since the Snecko Eye commonly results in unspent Energy at the end of turns, it works very well with Ice Cream to conserve that Energy. This also means that you can ignore playing high-cost and low-value cards, even if you do have enough Energy to play them.
-  Snecko Eye is very strong with many cards; among them are Bullet Time, Nightmare , Meteor Strike and Bludgeon. These cards take advantage of Snecko Eye's randomized cost and draw in varying ways.
-  Snecko Eye doesn't affect cards that cost X, which can make them a bit more valuable as it provides a place to spend Energy that the Snecko Eye would otherwise waste.
-  Cards with variable cost (Masterful Stab, Blood for Blood, and Force Field) will have their costs replaced by randomized costs when drawn. Their cost-reducing effects only apply if the conditions are newly met while they are in your hand (e.g. Blood for Blood will have its cost randomized when drawn, but that new cost can be reduced if you lose HP while it is in your hand). Their costs will be randomized again when next drawn.
-  Corruption overrides Confused entirely, and Skills will always cost 0.

## Strange Spoon
The Strange Spoon is a Shop Relic. When playing a card that Exhausts itself, it will instead be sent to the discard pile as normal 50% of the time.

    1.  Mechanics

-  Cards Exhausted through effects other than their own will never be saved. Likewise, Ethereal cards will not be saved at the end of a turn when they Exhaust themselves. Deus Ex Machina is Unplayable, and its effect to Exhaust itself when drawn does not count as playing it, so it will not be saved.

-  Cards played by Havoc and Omniscience temporarily gain the "Exhaust" effect, so the Strange Spoon can save these cards. However, if the card to be played is Unplayable (e.g. Wound, Tactician), it will Exhaust without being played, preventing the Strange Spoon from triggering.

-  As of version 2.0, Strange Spoon interacts oddly with Omniscience. If the card played by Omniscience is saved by Strange Spoon, it will have the Exhaust effect for the rest of the combat, and will try to Exhaust every time it is played. Havoc does not have this issue.

-  Although the in-game description claims that saved cards will discard, this is not entirely true. Instead, the card is simply placed in the discard pile as though it did not have the effect to Exhaust itself. This means that discard-related effects (e.g. Hovering Kite) will not trigger when the Strange Spoon prevents a card from Exhausting.

-  Works with Corruption, allowing cards that cost 0 to be preserved, and played multiple times for free.

    1.  Tips

-  Remember that taking this relic isn't always advantageous.
  -  Some effects (e.g. Dark Embrace, Dead Branch) reward you for Exhausting cards, and won't trigger if the Strange Spoon saves them.
  -  Other cards (e.g. Shiv, Pride) you'll want to Exhaust, rather than having them remain to clutter your deck.
  -  Statuses played with Medical Kit and Curses played with Blue Candle may be discarded instead of Exhausted.
-  Because Apparition Exhausts when played and is incredibly powerful, it may be worthwhile to invest in buying a Strange Spoon if you have Apparitions, or in taking the Apparitions if you have Strange Spoon already.

    1.  Trivia
- This relic's flavor text references a popular stage magic trick known as spoon bending.
  - The practice itself was popularized by the magician Uri Geller, and since been associated with psychokinetic or paranormal abilities. It has been referenced in popular culture many time, such as a scene in The Matrix when a young boy bends a spoon to demonstrate the unreality of a computer simulation, and the evolutionary line of the Psychic-type Pokémon Abra, Kadabra, and Alakazam, who utilize spoons to amplify their psychokinetic capabilities.

## Sundial
The Sundial is an Uncommon Relic. Every 3 times you shuffle your draw pile, you immediately gain 2 Energy.

Every time your discard pile is shuffled into your draw pile, including by effects such as Deep Breath and Reboot, the Sundial gains 1 charge. Charges persist between combats.

The Sundial is arguably one of the stronger uncommon relics; it's very powerful in many infinite damage combos. In addition, it is activated more often in thinner decks, similarly to The Abacus.

The general requirement for an infinite using the Sundial is:

-  Have a deck with few enough cards that the entire deck can be held in hand at once
-  Be able to do the following repeatedly using 2 or less Energy:
  -  Draw 1+ cards, 3 times (example: Pommel Strike+ or Shrug It Off). Essentially, the player needs 2 cards, with a total energy cost of 2 or less, that draw 3 cards between themselves.
  -
  -  Deal any amount of damage

This works because the Sundial has a weird property when combined with cards that draw multiple cards at once. If the player has an empty draw pile, a single card in their discard pile, and they play an effect that draws 2 cards, the draw pile will be shuffled twice. This is what allows Pommel Strike+, Shrug It Off and Sundial to go infinite, for example.

## The Boot
The Boot is a Common Relic. Anytime an Attack would deal 4 or less unblocked damage, it deals 5 damage instead.

The effect applies only to damage dealt by Attacks. It will not increase damage from other relics (Mercury Hourglass, Charon's Ashes), non-Attack cards that deal damage (Fire Breathing, Poison), or the Defect's Lightning and Dark Orbs.

    1.  Interactions
- Positive
  - Riddle with Holes and Pummel deal much more damage, since they are many small hits.
  - The Boot bypasses enemies' innate damage reduction, such as Byrd's Flight and Nemesis's Intangible.
  - Decks which utilize multiple low-damage 0 Energy Attacks (Shiv, Go for the Eyes) can especially benefit.
- Negative
  - Gaining Strength will quickly make The Boot obsolete.
  - Inflicting Vulnerable also makes The Boot obsolete.
  - If an Attack would deal 0 damage due to negative Strength, The Boot will not increase the damage to 5.
  - Using Body Slam while having 0 Block will also occur in The Boot not increasing damage to 5.

## Tungsten Rod
flavor = It's very very heavy.The Tungsten Rod is a rare Relic that reduces all health loss by 1. This includes health loss inflicted by damage, cards, and events.

    1.  Interactions
-  Torii: Torii activates before Tungsten Rod:
  -  Incoming damage > 5: Torii does not activate; Tungsten Rod reduces damage by 1.

-  Intangible: This buff reduce all damage and HP loss taken to 1 and then Tungsten Rod reduce it to 0, effectively give you immunity to all damage and HP loss.

If the amount of health lost is reduced to 0, the following effects still trigger:

- Static Discharge

Conversely, the following effects will not trigger:

- Rupture
- Emotion Chip
- Centennial Puzzle
- Runic Cube
- Self-Forming Clay

## Wrist Blade
class = SilentAttacks that cost 0 Energy deal 4 additional damage.

It affects any 0 cost Attack cards, regardless of class. As a result, cards like Anger, Claw, or Flurry of Blows when obtained from Prismatic Shard work with this relic.

It also applies to cards that have had their cost reduced to 0, such as cards with cost changed by Confused, Blood for Blood, Eviscerate, Streamline, or cards Retained under Establishment.

    1.  Synergies

-  Shivs are the most obvious use for this relic, being a passive and sizable buff, however it applies to natural 0-cost cards like Neutralize as well.
-  Bullet Time reduces all card costs in hand to 0 for the turn, giving ALL attacks 4 extra damage.
-  Setup and Forethought reduce the cost of one card to 0 until played, which can be used on an attack to buff it. (In the case of Forethought+, can be used on your entire hand)
-  Snecko Eye may reduce cost to 0 when you draw an attack.
-  Attack Potion sets the cost of the given attack to 0 for the turn.
-  Liquid Memories sets the cost of the retrieved card to 0 for the turn.

-  Swivel sets your next attack cost to 0.

    1.  Trivia
- This relic is a reference to Assassin's Creed as supported by the quote "Handy for assassinations".
