## Bolts Longs
data11=
Bolts [Longs] are ammunition used with Crossbows.

After researching Crossbow Bolts and learning the Blueprint (that can be bought occasionally in World's End), these bolts can be made on an Arrow Making Bench for 0.5 steel bars per Bolts [Longs]. They can later be stored in a Boltbox.

These bolts are the rarest bolts in Kenshi, as most of the ranger shops on the map do not sell them. Available places to buy large quantities of these bolts are the ranger shops in World's End and Mongrel. They can also be found as rare loot in several ruins.

## Crossbows
Crossbows are Kenshi's only ranged weapons that can be carried. To use a crossbow, equip the crossbow, make sure that the character has the correct ammunition in their inventory, and ensure that RANGED is active under JOBS.

Crossbows can do a decent amount of damage to a single target and cause significant bleeding. It is important to note that the size of the target (and its hitbox) plays an important role in the accuracy of crossbows. As such, large animals are easier to hit than humanoids, but so also it's more difficult to hit small targets like spiders. Players should to be cautious using crossbow characters and especially positioning them with particular care during fights, it's not rare for high-powered crossbows to kill weaker enemies, and so too allies succumb to friendly fire, outright and permanently.

NOTE: There is an intentional mechanic from the developers that makes characters with the Diplomat tag "immune" to crossbows. Attempting to shoot units who have a Diplomatic status will usually result in your character aiming at someone else entirely. It is possible to have your marksmen target sometimes glitching and instead hitting an invisible target behind the Diplomat you set to attack. Despite the immunity, your marksmen can have a chance of 'accidentally' hitting the Diplomatic character in front of the unit you are aiming at. On occasion (Sometimes after stealing something from them, assassination attempt etc...) you will be able to shoot to Diplomatic targets with a Crossbow.

Crossbows can be crafted through the Crossbow Smith stat.

See Turrets for their immobile counterparts.

    1.  Crossbow Stat
Stats Affected = -Accuracy-Rate of fire-Reload speed
The Crossbow stat affects the accuracy, rate of fire, and reload speed of a crossbow user, allowing more skilled crossbowmen to shoot faster and with greater accuracy the more they use crossbows in combat.

Certain Light Armour pieces can be worn to directly increase a character's Crossbow stat.
-  Assassin's Rags (1.10x) (Also increases Dexterity by 20%, making Crossbows even more effective)
-  Drifters Leather Jacket (1.10x)
-  Ninja Rags (1.10x)

A Steady Arm (left) can be worn as a Robot Limb, but it will only provide a bonus at [High] grade or above.

## Eagle's Cross
data2 = Bolts [Longs]
Eagle's Cross is a Crossbow with furthest range and highest damage of any ranged weapon. It can be dangerous in the hands of an unskilled shooter; a single friendly fire shot could kill any low Toughness allies.

It is the only Crossbow which requires Long Bolts which only hold 10 shots and weigh 2kg each stack. On long adventures a smaller crossbow may be more suitable due to this. The primary use for an Eagle's Cross is to eliminate targets from far distances and to deal with opponents who wear armour with high harpoon resistance.

After unlocking the ability to craft through use of a blueprint, players can craft Eagle's Crosses at a Crossbow Crafting Bench. A single Eagle's Cross is made up of 8 Steel Bars and 4 Crossbow Parts.

## Spring Bat
data3 = 6 kg
Spring Bat is a crossbow which uses heavy bolts. It inflicts heavy damage and is very effective even at low levels, but comes with the risk of a deadly misfire against a teammate due to its low accuracy and high damage.

It is a common weapon of choice by Escaped Servants and Scavengers.
