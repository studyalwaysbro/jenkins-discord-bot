## Dugong
The Dugong is a scout class submarine. Its small size, maneuverability and straightforward layout make it a good choice for novice players but for higher difficulties, a more powerful submarine is recommended. Priced at 6000 Marks, the Dugong is one of two default subs available at the start of a single player campaign, the other being the Barsuk.

= Notable Features =
The Dugong's price point is very low, making it available to purchase at the start of a Single Player Campaign.

The Dugong lacks any Fabricator, Medical Fabricator or Deconstructor, making it less self sufficient compared to other vanilla submarines.

= Layout =
The Dugong's command room is situated close to the rear, next to the engine room. In addition to the navigation terminal, it has two periscopes that are used to control two coilguns placed in the bottom-center and top-rear of the submarine.

In the front area of the main deck are a medical and weapons storerooms. The weapons storeroom can be opened by any crewmember, but a secure steel cabinet that contains the more powerful weaponry requires a captain's or a security officer's ID card.

The bottom deck has a reactor room, electrical room and a compartment for the oxygen generator. Two ballast tanks are also located on the bottom deck, one in the rear and one in the front.

The Dugong has one airlock, located on the top deck. Diving equipment is stored below it on the main deck.

= Trivia =
-  The Dugong was introduced in 0.9.
-  The Dugong's small ballast tanks are unable to fully counteract the weight of its airlock if left open, rendering the submarine being deadlocked until someone or something recycles the airlock.

## Hull
Hulls are rectangular areas in which water will not spawn. They are used to define areas which are inside the submarine, and also to designate rooms within the submarine for the Status Monitor and command callouts.

In order for a Hull to be watertight it must be fully enclosed by other structural objects such as internal walls and shells. A vent linked to an oxygen generator must be used to fill a Hull with oxygen.

    1.  Submarine Editor
The Hull object is found in the Structure category of objects, and can be made visible or invisible via the Visibility Options.

    1.  Gaps
Gaps are another object found in the Structure category, and are the means by which air and water can move between Hulls. Doors and Hatches come with a predefined Gap as part of the object.
