## Colorless Cards
Colorless cards are available to all characters. These cards are generally not gained from normal card reward screens--adventurers must instead acquire them through a combination of merchant shops, events, relic effects, and card effects. All colorless cards provide a beneficial effect ranging from mildly to extremely powerful.

The Merchant always has two colorless cards in his stock. They are always Uncommon or Rare. Obtaining the  Prismatic Shard allows those colorless cards to appear in normal card reward screens as well.

All the other colorless cards, despite sharing a grey border with Common cards, are all special cards that can only be obtained from certain events, or generated by certain cards and relics.

## Curse
Curses are negative cards. Unlike Status cards, they stay in your deck outside of combat. Under normal circumstances, all curses are unplayable, and many impose an additional negative effect.

Curses can only Transform into other curses. When an effect (such as Cursed Key or Transform) generates a random curse, it can only become one of the following:
-  Clumsy
-  Decay
-  Doubt
-  Injury
-  Normality
-  Pain
-  Parasite
-  Regret
-  Shame
-  Writhe
The above curses and Pride are the only ones that can be removed from the deck.

    1.  Interactions

    1.  Card
Numbers in parentheses are for upgraded cards.
-  Fire Breathing - Whenever you draw a Status or Curse card, deal 6(10) damage to all enemies.

    1.  Relics
-  Omamori - Negates the next 2 Curses you obtain.
-  Blue Candle - Lets you play Curses, Exhausting the card at the cost of 1 HP.
-  Darkstone Periapt - Increases your Max HP by 6 whenever you obtain a Curse.
-  Du-Vu Doll - Starts each combat with 1 additional Strength for each Curse in your deck.
-  Cursed Key - Gives you a Curse whenever you open a Chest.

    1.  Events
Numbers in parentheses are for Ascension 15 or higher.
-  The Divine Fountain - Lets you remove every removable Curse from the deck.
-  Match and Keep - Has 1(2) pair(s) of a random Curse available for matching.
-  Bonfire Spirits - Offering a Curse rewards you with Spirit Poop.

## Status
Statuses are negative cards that are shuffled into your deck during combat. However, unlike Curses, they will be removed at the end of combat. Under normal circumstances, most statuses are Unplayable (with the exception of Slimed), and some impose an additional negative effect. They cannot be upgraded, with the exception of Burn.

Status cards can be added to your deck either through Monster attacks, or through an effect on a card or a Relic.

    1.  Interactions
- Evolve: Whenever you draw a Status, draw 1(2) card(s).
- Fire Breathing: Whenever you draw a Status or Curse card, deal 6(10) damage to all enemies.
- Medical Kit: Lets you play Statuses, which will Exhaust them.

    1.  Relics that create Status cards
-  Mark of Pain: At the start of combat, add 2 Wounds to your draw pile.

    1.  Cards that create Status cards

-  Ironclad
  -  Wild Strike: Deal 12(17) damage. Shuffle a Wound into your draw pile.
  -  Reckless Charge: Deal 7(10) damage. Shuffle a Dazed into your draw pile.
  -  Power Through: Add 2 Wounds to your hand. Gain 15(20) Block.
  -  Immolate: Deal 21(28) damage to ALL enemies. Add a Burn into your discard pile.
-  Defect
  -  TURBO: Gain 2(3) . Add a Void into your discard pile.
  -  Overclock: Draw 2(3) cards. Add a Burn into your discard pile.

    1.  Enemies that create Status cards

    1.  Monsters

-  Acid Slime M and L - When using their Corrosive Spit (1 and 2 Slimeds to discard pile, respectively).
-  Spike Slime M and L - When using their Flame Tackle (1 and 2 Slimeds to discard pile, respectively).
-  Chosen - When the player uses non-attack cards while afflicted with Chosen's Hex debuff (1 Dazed to draw pile).
-  Sentry (Sentry & Spheric Guardian fight; Act 2 Hard Pool) - When using their Bolt (2/3 Dazeds to discard pile).
-  Orb Walker - When using its Laser (1 Burn to draw and discard piles).
-  Repulsor - When using its Repulse (2 Dazeds to draw pile).

    1.  Elites

-  Three Sentries- When using their Bolt (2/3 Dazeds to discard pile).
-  Book of Stabbing - From their Painful Stabs power when the player takes unblocked attack damage (1 Wound to discard pile each time).
-  Taskmaster - When using their Scouring Whip every turn (1/2/3 Wounds to discard pile).
-  Nemesis - When using their "Debuff" (3/5 Burns to discard pile).
-  Reptomancer's Dagger - When using their Stab (1 Wound to discard pile).
-  Spire Spear - When using their Burn Strike (2 Burns to discard pile/TOP of draw pile).

    1.  Bosses

## Upgrade
Every card in the game, aside from Curses and Statuses (with the exception of Burn), can be upgraded to a better version.

Cards can only be upgraded once, with the exception of Searing Blow.

Ordinarily, this is done by the "Smith" command in Rest sites.

Multiple Relics can upgrade cards.

This can also be done in a multitude of Events.

    1.  Upgrade-based Cards
-  Armaments: Directly upgrade 1 card in your hand for this combat. Armaments+ upgrades all cards in your hand for that combat.
-  Searing Blow: Can be upgraded an infinite number of times. The first upgrade gives 4 extra damage. Each further upgrade gives 1 more damage than the previous upgrade.
-  Lesson Learned: If this kills a non-minion enemy, upgrades 1 random card in your deck (permanently).  Does not upgrade the card for the current combat--you cannot use the upgraded version until next combat.
-  Master Reality: Whenever a card is created during combat, upgrade it.
-  Apotheosis: Upgrade ALL cards in your deck for this combat.
    1.  Relics that Upgrade Cards
-  Frozen Egg: All Power cards gained will be automatically upgraded.
-  Molten Egg: All Attack cards gained will be automatically upgraded.
-  Toxic Egg: All Skill cards gained will be automatically upgraded.
-  War Paint: On pickup, upgrades 2 random Skills.
-  Whetstone: On pickup, upgrades 2 random Attacks.
-  Tiny House: On pickup, upgrades 1 random card.
-  Warped Tongs: At the start of every turn, upgrades a random card in your hand for that combat.
-  Astrolabe: upon pickup transform 3 cards, then upgrade them.

    1.  Events that offers Cards upgrade
-  All acts
  -  Designer In-Spire
  -  Ominous Forge
  -  Upgrade Shrine
-  Act 1
  -  Living Wall
  -  Shining Light
-  Act 2
  -  Ancient Writing
    -  Only upgrades all of your Strikes and Defends
-  Act 3
  -  Mind Bloom
    -  Upgrades all of your cards, but gives you Mark of the Bloom, which prevents future healing.
    1.  Notes
