## Topology — Topological Spaces
A topology τ on X is a collection of subsets (open sets) containing ∅ and X, closed under arbitrary unions and finite intersections. Basis: collection B where every open set is union of basis elements. Examples: standard topology on R (open intervals), discrete (all subsets), indiscrete ({∅, X}), lower limit (Sorgenfrey line). Subspace topology: intersect open sets with subspace. Product topology: generated by products of open sets. Quotient topology: U open iff preimage under quotient map is open.

## Topology — Continuity and Homeomorphisms
f: X -> Y continuous iff preimage of every open set is open. Homeomorphism: bijective, continuous, with continuous inverse — topological equivalence. Topological invariants preserved by homeomorphisms: compactness, connectedness, Hausdorff property, fundamental group. Examples: (0,1) ≅ R, coffee cup ≅ torus (genus 1). Embedding: injective continuous map that is homeomorphism onto image. Separation axioms: T1 (points are closed), T2/Hausdorff (distinct points have disjoint neighborhoods), T3/regular, T4/normal. Urysohn's lemma: normal space, disjoint closed sets can be separated by continuous function.

## Topology — Compactness and Connectedness
Compact: every open cover has finite subcover. In Hausdorff spaces: compact sets are closed. Tychonoff's theorem: product of compact spaces is compact (requires axiom of choice). Connected: no separation into disjoint nonempty open sets. Path-connected: any two points joined by continuous path. Path-connected implies connected but not conversely. Components: maximal connected subspaces. Locally compact: every point has compact neighborhood. One-point compactification: adds point at infinity. Alexandroff compactification of R^n is S^n.

## Topology — Fundamental Group and Covering Spaces
Fundamental group π_1(X, x_0): homotopy classes of loops based at x_0. Group operation: concatenation. π_1(S^1) ≅ Z (winding number). π_1(S^n) = 0 for n ≥ 2. π_1(T^2) ≅ Z × Z. Van Kampen's theorem: π_1 of union computed from pieces. Covering space: p: E -> X where every point has evenly covered neighborhood. Lifting: paths and homotopies lift uniquely given starting point. Deck transformations: automorphisms of covering space. Universal cover: simply connected covering space. Classification: coverings of X correspond to subgroups of π_1(X).

## Discrete Math — Combinatorics
Counting principles: addition (or), multiplication (and). Permutations: P(n,k) = n!/(n-k)!. Combinations: C(n,k) = n!/[k!(n-k)!]. Binomial theorem: (x+y)^n = Σ C(n,k) x^k y^{n-k}. Stars and bars: distributing n identical objects into k bins = C(n+k-1, k-1). Inclusion-exclusion: |A∪B| = |A| + |B| - |A∩B|, generalizes to n sets. Pigeonhole principle: n+1 pigeons in n holes means some hole has ≥ 2. Derangements: D_n = n! Σ_{k=0}^{n} (-1)^k/k! ≈ n!/e. Catalan numbers: C_n = C(2n,n)/(n+1), count balanced parentheses, binary trees, etc.

## Discrete Math — Graph Theory
Graph G = (V, E). Degree: number of edges at vertex. Handshaking lemma: Σ deg(v) = 2|E|. Eulerian circuit: uses every edge exactly once, exists iff all vertices have even degree. Hamiltonian cycle: visits every vertex exactly once (NP-complete to decide). Trees: connected acyclic graphs, |E| = |V| - 1. Planar graphs: can be drawn without edge crossings. Euler's formula: V - E + F = 2. Kuratowski: planar iff no K_5 or K_{3,3} subdivision. Chromatic number: minimum colors for proper coloring. Four color theorem: planar graphs are 4-colorable. Matching: set of disjoint edges. König's theorem: in bipartite graphs, max matching = min vertex cover.

## Discrete Math — Number Theory
Division algorithm: a = bq + r, 0 ≤ r < b. GCD: Euclidean algorithm. Bézout's identity: gcd(a,b) = ax + by for some integers x,y. Fundamental theorem of arithmetic: unique prime factorization. Modular arithmetic: a ≡ b (mod n) iff n | (a-b). Fermat's little theorem: a^{p-1} ≡ 1 (mod p) for prime p, gcd(a,p)=1. Euler's theorem: a^{φ(n)} ≡ 1 (mod n). Chinese remainder theorem: system of congruences with coprime moduli has unique solution. RSA: n = pq, encrypt m^e mod n, decrypt c^d mod n where ed ≡ 1 (mod φ(n)).

## Discrete Math — Recurrences and Generating Functions
Linear recurrence: a_n = c_1 a_{n-1} + ... + c_k a_{n-k}. Characteristic equation: r^k - c_1 r^{k-1} - ... - c_k = 0. Distinct roots: a_n = Σ α_i r_i^n. Repeated root r with multiplicity m: terms r^n, nr^n, ..., n^{m-1}r^n. Ordinary generating function: F(x) = Σ a_n x^n. Operations: shifting (multiply by x), convolution (multiply series). Fibonacci: F(x) = x/(1-x-x^2). Exponential generating function: Σ a_n x^n/n!. Useful for labeled structures. Master theorem for divide-and-conquer: T(n) = aT(n/b) + f(n).
