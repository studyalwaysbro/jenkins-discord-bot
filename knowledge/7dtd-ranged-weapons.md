is an Agility-based Tier 3 Handgun succeeding the .44 Magnum in 7 Days to Die.

    1.  In-Game Description
This heavy pistol uses .44 Magnum ammo.

Repair with a Repair Kit.Scrap to Handgun Parts.

    1.  Overview
The ' is a short to medium range semi-automatic handgun, which can be crafted or found throughout the world in containers, such as weapon bags, gun safes, toilets, and loot bags. It is the single shot, high damage alternative to the SMG-5.

The  requires .44 Magnum bullets and will hold 8 bullets. It reloads automatically, provided the correct Ammunition is available. It can also be reloaded manually at any time using the reload key (defaults to R) provided the magazine is not full.

    1.  Combat
The  is step up from the .44 Magnum and is capable of dispatching some Ferals with a single shot. It carries many of the same traits as its predecessor and, as such, players focused on a stealth build (Hidden Strike and From The Shadows) can still use the Silencer Mod to quietly clear out a Point of Interest (POI) without being overwhelmed, even high tier POIs on high difficulty settings. The added bonus of being able to equip a Magazine Extender Mod allows the player to deal with larger groups compared to the .44 Magnum. However, due to its relatively low magazine capacity even with the mod,  is still unsuitable for crowd control, especially during Blood Moon Hordes.

## .44 Magnum
is an Agility-based Tier 2 Handgun succeeding the Pistol and preceding the .44 Desert Vulture in 7 Days to Die.

    1.  In-Game Description
The  is the king of handguns. It's the most powerful but makes the most noise. Wield this bad boy and make Clint proud.

Repair with a Repair Kit.Scrap to Handgun Parts.

    1.  Overview
The ' is a short to medium range double action revolver which can be found throughout the world, more often at Shotgun Messiah Gun Stores or crafted. This weapon requires the .44 Magnum Bullet and will hold six cartridges. It can be reloaded manually at any time using the reload key (defaults to R) provided the cylinder is not full.

    1.  Combat
The  is powerful Weapon that can easily dispatch the toughest Zombies with ease. At close range, it typically only takes a single shot to the head to kill non-Feral or -Radiated tough zombies, and one shot to the body to kill non-Feral or -Radiated normal zombies. It comes with several major drawbacks however:
-  1) It delivers a very loud report, attracting nearby zombies to the source

-  2) The ammunition it uses is quite rare, which compels its users to obtain a Workbench to craft the ammunition

-  3) It only holds 6 cartridges in its cylinder at a time and subsequently requires frequent reloading

-  4) Fairly long reload time of 4 seconds

All of the drawbacks above makes the  unsuitable for crowd control, especially Blood Moon Hordes. But because of its high alpha damage, players focused on a stealth build (Hidden Strike and From The Shadows) can use the Silencer Mod to quietly clear out a Point of Interest (POI) without being overwhelmed, even mid tier POIs on high difficulty settings.

## AK-47 Machine Gun
The AK-47 Machine Gun, or simply AK-47, is a Fortitude-based Machine Gun in 7 Days to Die.

    1.  Description
The AK-47 is a short- to medium-ranged automatic weapon, affected by the Machine Gunner perk. Similar to other machine guns and rifles, the AK-47 uses 7.62mm Rounds as Ammunition. Previously named the AK-47 Assault Rifle and classified as a rifle, the AK-47 is now considered a machine gun in terms of Perks. It has the highest damage output potential out of all the conventional firearms, that lends the AK 47 well to being the player's backup gun to defend against surprise threats while scavenging or collecting resources.

    1.  Combat
Due to the AK-47 having a lower head shot multiplier than other 7.62mm rifles, it is less effective in situations where time-to-kill is not as important as conservation of ammunition, such as in base defense. It takes two shots to the head to kill a normal Zombie, three shots to kill a bloated Zombie, or several shots to the body or limbs.

    1.  Crafting

    1.  Repair
The condition of an AK-47 deteriorates with each use until it will eventually stop working and will need to be repaired. This can be done using a Repair Kit.

Pipe Rifles were added to the game in Alpha 20. They are intended to be an early-game weapon. The two core components used in crafting are pipes and glue.

Like all pipe weapons, it can be crafted right at the beginning of the game (assuming the player has all its crafting ingredients) requiring no workstations, books or skills.

It's a good weapon if the player is good enough to use the rifle effectively, and while collecting supplies to craft the better, superior Hunting Rifle unless it's found in the world prior to that.

## Pump Shotgun
1.  Overview
The ' is a pump-action firearm that can be found/looted or crafted in 7 Days to Die. The Shotgun can use either Shotgun Shells or Shotgun Slugs as Ammunition and when empty will reload automatically or manually using the reload key (default key 'R').

    1.  Combat
In close combat situations, the  is tremendously powerful and deals a high amount of damage to enemies. It has a slight spread of the shot which allows the Weapon to hit multiple targets at once; a shotgun choke may be attached to tighten the buckshot spread, reducing spray but concentrating the damage per shell. Alternatively, a sawed-off shotgun barrel may be used instead to achieve the opposite effect, increasing spray to hit more targets; the duck bill attachment changes the spread to a more horizontal pattern. The damage is calculated if all the 8 pellets hit.

A major drawback for the shotgun is the low ammo capacity, particularly due to its use for close range combat. ## Wooden Bow
1.  Description
The Wooden Bow is the second craftable bow in 7 Days to Die. It is a medium-range weapon that fires arrows as ammunition. Wooden Bow can be used with five different types of arrows: Stone Arrow, Iron Arrow, Steel Arrow, Flaming Arrow, and Exploding Arrow. To equip a different type of ammo than the default stone arrows, press and hold R, then select the ammo type you want. The damages given are based on values of the different types of arrows that the bow can use. The bow itself (without arrows) can not cause any damage.

Sneaking attack increases 100% extra damage in A21.

    1.  Combat
During combat, a Wooden Bow is a powerful medium to long range weapon. At short range, however, the player may have trouble due to the time it takes to knock an additional arrow. Due to its slow firing rate, a bow should be used with care at short range against more than one enemy or wild animal. Bows are great for stealth attacks, as firing an arrow doesn't make a sound and won't alert nearby zombies. Hitting a zombie without killing him, does alert the zombie.

Aiming the bow takes skill. The player must take into account the time of flight of an arrow and the effect of gravity upon it. Arrows, unlike bullets, do not travel in a straight line, and have limited range. Aiming slightly above the head, depending on range, usually guarantees a hit.

Arrows (like bolts) have a chance to apply a Arrow Critical Bleedout debuff, which drains 1 health per second for 50s.
