Watcher cards are gained from Card Reward screens during games played with the Watcher. They can also be gained from merchant shops and from the effects of events, relics, and other Watcher cards. Watcher cards come in three rarities: common, uncommon, and rare.

## Alpha
The Alpha card is the first of a set of 3 cards (Alpha, Beta, Omega) to generate the Omega buff, which deals 50(60) damage per turn to all enemies.

Playing the Alpha card will shuffle a Beta into your Draw Pile. Playing the Beta card will in turn shuffle an Omega card into your draw pile, and playing the Omega card will generate the final Omega buff.

Alpha is the only way to generate a Beta and eventually Omega cards. Beta and Omega cannot be produced by Dead Branch or other card generating cards or potions, although the Alpha card can.

    1.  Interactions

- If the Master Reality power is active, Alpha's generated Beta card will be upgraded (from 2 to 1 Energy), and the eventual Omega card will also be upgraded (increase from 50 to 60 damage).

## Battle Hymn
stacking_behavior = Intensity

Battle Hymn adds a Smite card to your hand at the start of each turn. The Smite cards are both created and Retained, opening room for synergy with some of the Watcher's power cards (examples below).

    1.  Interactions

- Establishment will reduce the Energy cost of each added Smite to 0 on the following turn.
- Master Reality will upgrade each Smite upon creation, increasing their damage.


- Since Smite cards are exhausted on use, Dead Branch will add a card to your deck for each Smite played.


- The constant addition of Smite cards can make triggering Signature Move difficult.

## Blasphemy
Blasphemy is a Rare Skill for the Watcher. When played, she enters Divinity immediately without needing to accumulate any Mantra. However, she gets the Blasphemer buff, which instantly kills her at the start of the player's next turn. The card is Exhausted upon use, meaning it can only be played once per battle unless the player has Exhume or the Strange Spoon.

Upgrading the card to Blasphemy+ causes it to Retain, allowing the player to keep it in their hand for the exact right turn and hand to finish the battle.

    1.  Strategy

- Obviously, if the player does not plan to end the fight the turn Blasphemy is used, it should not be played unless the player has Intangible, Buffer, or another source of death prevention such as a Fairy in a Bottle.
- Blasphemy synergizes extremely well with cards such as Signature Move due to its ability to instantly allow the Watcher to enter Divinity, which will triple output damage.
  - Blasphemy also synergizes well with low-cost cards that either deal damage or draw other cards, such as Cut Through Fate and Sands of Time, as Divinity lasts for one turn, meaning drawing cheap cards with decent attack power is a boon.
- By far, the worst cards to use with Blasphemy are Vault, and Meditate, as they immediately end your turn for setting up the next turn. For all intents and purposes, there is no next turn.

    1.  Interactions
stacking_behavior = none
- Since the Blasphemer effect is technically a buff, it cannot be negated by Artifact, nor can it be cleansed by Orange Pellets.
- Since the damage is taken at the start of the next turn, rather than the end of the current turn, Intangible can only prevent it if you will have it next turn (either by having at least two stacks, or by having Incense Burner's counter at 5).

## Bowling Bash
Bowling Bash is a Common Attack card for the Watcher. It deals 7 damage for each enemy present in combat. When Upgraded, it deals an additional 3 damage for each enemy in combat, for a total of 10 damage per enemy.

    1.  Interactions

-  Highly effective against encounters with multiple enemies (e.g Sentries, Gremlin Leader, Reptomancer).
- Only enemies that are currently alive in the battle count.
  -  Dead Darklings do not count.

    1.  Trivia
- When this card is played while 3 or more enemies are present, a sound of bowling pins falling will play.
- This card may be a reference to a skill of the same name in the MMO Ragnarok Online.

## Brilliance
Brilliance deals 12(16) damage with one additional damage added for each Mantra gained over the course of the battle.

With a cost of only 1 Energy, a build equipped for reliably entering Divinity can rapidly accrue significant damage at low cost.

For a list of cards and relics which increase Mantra and add to Brilliance's damage, see the Mantra page.

## Conclude
Conclude is an Attack card for the Watcher It deals 12 damage to all enemies, and then ends your current turn when played. As an Uncommon card, it appears as a reward for combat, although less often than Commons.

Upon upgrade, it deals 4 additional damage, for a total of 16 damage to all enemies.

    1.  Interactions

-  This will always be the last card you play on your turn, which prevents you from playing another card after Conclude
-  Be wary when used with the Wrath stance, as once you use its doubled damage on Conclude, you will be forced to take double damage if the enemy is attacking that turn.
-  When used with a Duplication Potion or Omniscience, it will instead end your turn before a second Conclude is played, essentially negating their effects.

The Expunger card created can be upgraded with Master Reality to deal 15 damage per energy, rather than 9.

## Consecrate
Consecrate is a common 0-cost Attack for The Watcher.  When played, it deals 5 damage to all enemies.  When Upgraded, it deals 8 damage instead.

## Crescendo
Crescendo is a common Skill for The Watcher.  When played, it causes the player to enter Wrath Stance.  It Retains itself so the player can wait to play it at a convenient time.  It also Exhausts itself so it can normally only be played once per combat.

Upgrading Crescendo causes it to cost 0 Energy.

## Crush Joints
Crush Joints is an Attack card for the Watcher. It deals 8 damage, and additionally applies 1 Vulnerable if the last card played was a Skill. As a Common card, it can be found as a reward after combats. Upon Upgrade, it grants an additional 2 damage and also applies 1 more Vulnerable, allowing it to deal 10 damage and apply 2 Vulnerable if played directly after playing a Skill.

## Cut Through Fate
Cut Through Fate is a common Attack for The Watcher.  It deals 7 damage to a target enemy, Scrys 2 cards, and then draws one card.  If Upgraded, deals 2 additional damage and scrys 1 additional card.

Note that in the scrying screen, the leftmost card that the player does not choose to discard will be the one drawn by Cut Through Fate.

## Deus Ex Machina
Deus Ex Machina adds 2 (3) Miracles to your hand when drawn.

    1.  Interactions

- If the Master Reality buff is active, Deus Ex Machina's added Miracle cards (Gain ) will upgrade to Miracle+ (Gain ).


-  Deus Ex Machina is only activated when the card is drawn. Adding Deus Ex Machina to your hand (such as with 1=Dead Branch or a Skill Potion) or placing it directly into your hand (such as with Secret Technique) will not activate the card.

## Deva Form
stacking_behavior = Intensity

Deva Form is a Rare Power card for the Watcher that grants the player the Deva buff, which increases the amount of Energy the player gets at the start of their turn by one every turn; in other words, once Deva Form is played, the player will have one more Energy to spend every turn afterwards.

    1.  Interactions

- Being a high-cost power card, Deva Form is a strong candidate for using alongside Omniscience.
- Conjure Blade can create extremely powerful Expungers using the excess energy generated by Deva Form.
- The energy drawback of Fasting is easily minimized by Deva Form.
- Deva Form, in general, makes X cost Cards much more accessible.

- The card art depicts the relics Singing Bowl, Vajra, and Juzu Bracelet.

## Devotion
stacking_behavior = Intensity
Devotion adds 2(3) Mantra at the start of each turn. This can provide significant aid in reaching the Divinity stance.

Devotion ultimately ends up being a niche inclusion to the mantra build, and it must be upgraded. Devotion is unfortunately very slow, but in longer fights (such as boss fights), it can be very powerful. It stacks very well with itself, and if you can somehow put 3 of these into play, you can enter Divinity every turn with ease.

    1.  Interactions

- Brilliance can quickly accrue high damage at low cost with Devotion's Mantra accumulation.
- Pairs well with Damaru for rapid Mantra accumulation.

## Eruption
Eruption is a starting card for the Watcher. It inflicts 9 damage, then switches the Watcher's Stance to Wrath. Upgrading Eruption lowers the Energy cost from 2 to 1.

Eruption cards cannot be given as combat rewards or purchased from the Merchant, but can be found in the Match and Keep event.

## Establishment
Establishment reduces the Energy cost of a Retained card by 1 for each turn it has been Retained. The cost reduction lasts until the end of the combat.

For example, if a Protect is Retained, on the next turn it will cost 1 energy, and after another turn it will cost 0.

Upgrading this card grants it the Innate keyword.

    1.  Interactions

-  Cards temporarily retained by Meditate will have their cost reduced by 1 on the following turn.
-  If carrying the Prismatic Shard to acquire cards of other colors, cards retained by Well-Laid Plans and Equilibrium will have their cost reduced on the following turn.


-  Cards held by Runic Pyramid do not count as retained cards, and so their cost is not affected by Establishment.

stacking_behavior = Intensity

## Evaluate
Evaluate is one of the Watcher's basic Block-generating Skills. It generates more Block than a starter Defend, and adds an Insight to your draw pile each time it's played. This means that over an entire shuffle, Evaluate will produce 6 (10) Block, -1 Energy +0 hand size, compared to Defend's 5 (8) Block, -1 Energy -1 hand size

Evaluate synergizes well with Master Reality, which will upgrade its Insight into Insight+, making the card's effect over a shuffle go from +0 hand size to +1 hand size.

Like other Insight-generating cards, Evaluate has an anti-synergy with restrictions on number of cards played, such as the Time Eater, Normality or Velvet Choker.

## Foreign Influence
Foreign Influence is an Uncommon Skill for the Watcher that presents you with 3 random Attack cards of any color (Including colorless) from which you may choose one to add to your hand (Adding one is not mandatory). Foreign Influence then Exhausts.

Upgrading Foreign Influence reduces the cost of the card it adds to your hand to 0 for that turn.

It can only generate a card that is available through shop and rewards with some exceptions: it cannot generate a healing card.

Foreign Influence does not grant an Orb slot, so attacks that generate orbs are less useful.

    1.  Excluded Cards
- Feed (Healing)
- Reaper (Healing)
- Lesson Learned (Potential for the player to exploit its permanent Upgrade)
- Bite (Healing and event-exclusive)
- Ritual Dagger (Event-exclusive)
- Shiv (Cards effect only)
- Smite (Cards effect only)
- Through Violence (Card effect only)

    1.  See Also

## Foresight
stacking_behavior = Intensity

Foresight is a simple, but very powerful card. By scrying 3 (or 4 when upgraded) before you draw for turn, you can get all of the cards you don't need out of the way. If you scry just one card into the discard pile that you were never going to use, you effectively drew an extra card that turn. Additionally, it can allow you to dig for a certain card. For example, if you want to draw an Eruption, and your deck has 8 cards in it, scrying 3 cards into the graveyard allows you to draw the Eruption 100% of the time. Given it's power, most Watcher decks should want exactly one copy of Foresight.

## Judgment
Judgment will instantly kill an enemy who has 30(40) or less HP. This bypasses the enemy's Block, concerning only their remaining HP.

## Lesson Learned
Lesson Learned will upgrade a random card in your deck if it is used to kill an enemy. After being used it exhausts, whether it has killed an enemy or not.

The kills must be considered Fatal to upgrade a card. Not all enemies count as Fatal kills (exceptions detailed below).

    1.  Interactions

- Lesson Learned is capable of upgrading itself.
- Exhume can be used to return Lesson Learned to your deck, allowing for more than one use or kill per battle.
- Strange Spoon gives a 50% chance that Lesson Learned will not exhaust on use.


- Minions do not count as Fatal kills. These are recognized by the  icon. For a list of minion enemies, see the Minion page.
- If fighting Darklings, reducing the HP of one of them to 0 does not count as a Fatal kill unless it was the final enemy from the batch.
- Defeating the first phase of the Awakened One does not count as a Fatal kill.
- The card it upgrades will not be upgraded during the fight itself, and only from the next fight onward.

## Master Reality
Master Reality is a Power card for the Watcher. When Master Reality's buff is active, any cards created during combat are automatically upgraded.

List of Watcher cards affected by Master Reality:
-  Smite cards created by Battle Hymn and Carve Reality
-  Through Violence created by Reach Heaven
-  Safety created by Deceive Reality
-  Expunger created by Conjure Blade
-  The card chosen through Foreign Influence
-  Miracles created by Deus Ex Machina
-  Insights created by Evaluate, Pray, and Study
-  Beta created by Alpha
-  Omega created by Beta
Cards generated from other classes may also be affected if they are somehow obtained, such as with Prismatic Shard. Notable examples include:

-  Shivs generated by Blade Dance or the many other cards which generate them
-  The card duplicated by Nightmare

    1.  Interactions

-  Playing an Alpha card after playing Master Reality will produce Beta+ (cost reduced from 2 to 1), which in turn will produce Omega+ (damage increased from 50 to 60).
-  Cards created via the 1=Dead Branch, Enchiridion, and Nilry's Codex will be upgraded once the Master Reality buff is active.
- Cards created by Colorless Cards such as Discovery, Metamorphosis, and Chrysalis will all be upgraded.
-  Cards that are returned from stasis into your hand from killing one of the Bronze Automaton's Bronze Orbs are upgraded by this card.

stacking_behavior = None

## Omniscience
Omniscience plays a card you select from your Draw Pile two times and then exhausts it from your deck. The energy cost of the targeted card is disregarded.

X-cost cards will be played with the Energy you have available after playing Omniscience.

    1.  Interactions

- Using Omniscience on another Omniscience will play the latter twice, allowing you to choose two more cards to then play twice, resulting in 4 cards played at once.
- Omniscience's exhaust mechanic is irrelevant for power cards, since power cards are removed from play after being used regardless.
- Since the Energy cost of the played card is disregarded, high-cost/high-yield cards such as Deva Form and Ragnarok are strong candidates for use.
- Playing Indignation twice will allow entering Wrath and then applying 3(5) Vulnerable to all enemies.
- Playing Inner Peace twice will allow entering Calm and then drawing 3(4) additional cards.
- Against Time Eater with Time Warp buff, playing Omniscience as the 12th card can bypass the card limit, Time Warp counter still progresses, but the forced end turn effect is ignored until all doubled cards are played, moreover, if playing Omniscience as the 12th into choosing other Omnisciences, then more cards could be played before forced to end turn.
- If during an Omniscience chain, an Omniscience is played as the 12th card, then it is equivalent to playing the Omniscience as the 12th card fresh from hand, ignoring any effects of previously played Omnisciences.


- Targeting a card that ends your turn will end your turn immediately, without playing a second copy.
- Activating a card whose conditions wouldn't work, such as Signature Move while you still have attack cards in your hand, will result in the card not activating.
- Any other restrictions on the number of cards you can play in a turn are not ignored by this. So playing an Omniscience as the third card, when a Normality is in hand will result in the card being exhausted without ever being played. The same is true for Velvet Choker.
- Against Time Eater with Time Warp buff, playing Omniscience as the 11th card cannot bypass the card limit unless another Omniscience is selected to be the 12th card play, that is, if the 11th card Omniscience selects Strike, only the first copy of Strike can be played.

## Perseverance
Perseverance is an uncommon Skill card for The Watcher. By default it grants the player 5 Block, or 7 if Upgraded. The card Retains itself, and whenever this card is Retained its amount of Block increases for the rest of combat.

    1.  Strategy

-  This card performs best in longer fights, as it can be Retained multiple turns in a row to increase the amount of Block it grants. In shorter fights, this card is not much better than a basic Defend because there are not as many opportunities to Retain it.

## Pray
Pray is, unfortunately, rarely worth taking. The issue with Pray is that it does literally nothing on the turn you cast it, unless you're entering Divinity. Additionally, because you had to draw Pray, then draw the Insight, just to draw 2 cards, the end result is that you didn't draw any cards. This makes Pray one of the worst cards in the game for a normal deck, unless you're incredibly committed to achieving Divinity. However, if you can assemble a small enough deck (less than 9 cards) that can somehow generate a lot of energy, a Pray+ can essentially re-draw itself and an Insight over and over.

Ultimately, one of the best ways to consider Divinity cards is to consider how many cycles through your deck it takes to achieve Divinity. For example, a card that gives 3 mantra takes 4 deck cycles to reach Divinity, while a card that gives 4 mantra would take 3 deck cycles. Thus, you should ultimately achieve to have your mantra cards give a total of either 5 or 10 mantra each deck cycle. Devotion and Damaru can also help with this. As an example of a great Divinity deck, a deck with an upgraded Pray, a Worship, and a Damaru can easily achieve 10 Mantra in just 2-3 turns.

Thus, you should ultimately achieve to have your mantra cards give a total of either 5 or 10 mantra each deck cycle. Devotion and Damaru can also help with this. As an example of a great Divinity deck, a deck with an upgraded Prostrate, a Worship, and a Damaru can easily achieve 10 Mantra in just 2-3 turns.

## Rushdown
Rushdown is a Power card for the Watcher. After it is played, you immediately draw 2 cards when entering the Wrath Stance.

When Upgraded, Rushdown's cost becomes 0.

    1.  Mechanics

-  The Rushdown buff is stackable, so playing multiple copies of Rushdown will draw even more cards upon entering Wrath.
-  Rushdown triggers after the card that enters Wrath is put into the discard pile.

    1.  Infinite Combos
Rushdown is a key component of Stance-based infinite combos. All that is required for an infinite combo, in addition to Rushdown itself, is:

-  A deck that is thin enough to hold all cards in hand
-  1 card that enters Wrath and 1 card that enters Calm, with these characteristics:
  -  They cost at most 2 Energy in total (or at most 3 Energy if you have Violet Lotus)
  -  They deal any amount of damage, or you have another damage source such as Panache or Letter Opener

Once the whole deck is held in hand, enter Calm, then enter Wrath. You will immediately draw the Calm and Wrath cards again and gain 2 Energy from exiting Calm.

    1.  Trivia

## Spirit Shield

## Swivel
stacking_behavior = CounterSwivel is a Skill for the Watcher. It gives 8 Block (11 if Upgraded), as well as 1 stack of the Free Attack Power buff, which makes the next Attack played cost 0 Energy.

    1.  Mechanics

-  Free Attack Power buff is a stackable buff that makes all Attacks cost 0, and 1 stack is removed each time an Attack is played. Multiple stacks can be gained by playing Swivel multiple times without playing any Attacks.
  - Even if the Attack played already costs 0 (e.g. Flurry of Blows), a stack of Free Attack Power is still used up.
-  Free Attack Power does not expire at the end of the turn.
- When using an Attack that costs X with Free Attack Power, X is still equal to current Energy. However, no Energy is spent after playing the card.

    1.  Tips

-  Swivel is already quite Energy-efficient when using it with an Attack that costs 1, since it gains Block and also attacks for a total of 2 Energy. It becomes much more powerful when used with high-cost Attacks, such as Ragnarok.

## Talk to the Hand
stacking_behavior = Intensity
Talk to the Hand is an Uncommon Watcher Attack card. It is a single target hit dealing a modest 5(7) damage which exhausts itself afterwards. However, it applies a debuff to the attacked enemy which returns 2(3) Block to the player on future attacks against that enemy. Since the block return debuff is based on each hit on the debuffed enemy, attacks which hit the debuffed enemy more than once or hit multiple debuffed enemies will give multiple instances of Block. This makes Talk to the Hand an unusual attack which usually serves to defend the player on the played turn and after, more than to deal any meaningful damage.

Talk to the Hand works well with multi-hitting cards like Flying Sleeves, Bowling Bash, Tantrum, Ragnarok, or Conjure Blade, as well as hand-returning cards such as Flurry of Blows or Weave. It also allows Wave of the Hand to apply its Weak debuff multiple times for extra defensive power.

The block effect, like all debuffs, is blocked by Artifact, and can be removed by bosses, such as The Champ and Time Eater, with debuff clearing actions.

## Vault
Vault is a Skill card for the Watcher. When played, it immediately ends the player's turn and the player starts a new turn (more precisely, skips the enemies' turn), the interaction of Vault with various buffs/debuffs does not follow a general rule and requires detailed explanations.

Without Vault, the order after the player clicks the end turn button is: player's turn ends->enemy's turn starts->enemy's turn->enemy's turn ends->round ends->player's turn starts, the part where Vault skipped is: enemy's turn starts->enemy's turn->enemy's turn ends->round ends, below is an exhaustive explanation of Vault interaction.

When the enemies' turn starts, enemies remove their Block, Byrd resets Flying, and Corrupt Heart resets Invincible, which are all prevented by Vault.

"End of Turn Trigger" buffs/debuffs trigger at the end of the player's or enemies' turn, when playing Vault, if the buff/debuff belongs to the player, then it triggers, if it belongs to the enemy, then it does not (for example, Regeneration does trigger, but Regenerate does not): Combust, Constricted, Equilibrium, Establishment, Shackled, Dexterity Down, Strength Down, Omega, Regenerate, Regeneration, Ritual (player version), Study, The Bomb, Well-Laid Plans, Wraith Form.

"End of Turn Decrease/Wear off" buffs/debuffs decrease/wear off at the end of the player's or enemies' turn, when playing Vault, if the buff/debuff belongs to the player, then it decreases/wears off, if it belongs to the enemy, then it does not: Amplify, Burst, Double Tap, Entangled, Shackled, Intangible (enemy version), Dexterity Down, Strength Down, No Draw, Rage, Rebound, Regeneration, The Bomb.

"End of Round" buffs/debuffs decrease/wear off/reset at the end of the enemies' turn, all are prevented by Vault, whether it belongs to the player or the enemies: Blur, Double Damage, Draw Reduction, Duplication, Equilibrium, Frail, Strength Up, Intangible (player version), Lock-On, Malleable, No Block, Ritual (enemy version), Slow, Vulnerable, Wave of the Hand, Weak.

    1.  Interactions

- Synergizes with powers that trigger at the start of your turn, such as Deva Form, Devotion, and Establishment.
- Scry will trigger for Vault's extra turn if Foresight has been played.
- Triggers the effects of Perseverance, Sands of Time, or Windmill Strike if any of those cards are in the player's hand when Vault is played.
- If Simmering Fury is played before using Vault, Wrath will be entered on Vault's extra turn.
- Wave of the Hand's effects carry over to Vault's extra turn.
- Can be used to deal additional damage against Giant Head since Slow is not reset at the start of the extra turn.
- If played as the 12th card against Time Eater, Time Eater still gains strength but the player gets another turn with the Time Warp clock reset to 0.
- When played whilst having Velvet Choker, it gives you a new turn, thus resetting the number of cards you have played back to 0.

## Wallop
1.  Tips
Wallop is useful for protecting oneself while in Wrath, as the block gained will scale to the increased damage. This makes it much better than playing regular Defend cards as well.

Using Wreath of Flame to increase the damage of this card also increases the Block gained from it. Remember that the effect of Vigor is doubled/tripled when in Wrath/Divinity. It also works well for this reason against Vulnerable enemies.

This is an excellent card for tackling enemies with Thorns, as the Block is received before the retaliation damage.

Avoid using this card against enemies with large amounts of Block, as you must deal unblocked damage to receive block in return.

It does not scale with Dexterity, as the block gained is always equal to the damage dealt. Instead, it scales twice with Strength, as each point increases the damage and block by one.

## Weave
The Weave card deals only a small amount of damage, but in a deck with many sources of scrying you can return every copy of Weave to your hand multiple times per turn. This can turn it into the backbone of your offense.

One important note is that if you choose to discard Weave while scrying, it will immediately be returned to your hand for free. Thus you should always choose to discard Weave, unless that would lead to your deck being reshuffled at an inconvenient time.

## Worship
Worship costs 2 energy, and generates 5 Mantra without doing anything else. Unlike Prostrate and Pray+, Worship is energy negative. However, it can allow you to enter Divinity through just two cycles of your deck, meaning Worship can be great as your only mantra-generating card. However, having this card be upgraded (which gives it Retain) is critical, as it means that it can be cast on turns where you have the energy, while also allowing you to enter Divinity at the perfect moment. Finally, if you're running Omniscience, choosing Worship allows you to instantly enter Divinity, and costs 0 energy if Omniscience is upgraded, which is an incredibly powerful combo.

Ultimately, one of the best ways to consider Divinity cards is to consider how many cycles through your deck it takes to achieve Divinity. For example, a card that gives 3 mantra takes 4 deck cycles to reach Divinity, while a card that gives 4 mantra would take 3 deck cycles. Thus, you should ultimately achieve to have your mantra cards give a total of either 5 or 10 mantra each deck cycle. Devotion and Damaru can also help with this. As an example of a great Divinity deck, a deck with an upgraded Prostrate, a Worship, and a Damaru can easily achieve 10 Mantra in just 2-3 turns.

## Wreath of Flame
stacking_behavior = Intensity
Wreath of Flame is a Skill card for the Watcher. It adds 5 stacks of the Vigor buff (8 stacks if  Upgraded), making the next Attack played deal additional damage per hit.

This effect is applied to every hit of an attack, and before multipliers, making it exceptionally powerful when stacked with multiplying effects:

-  Wrath
-  Divinity
-  Vulnerable
-  Multi-hit attacks (e.g: Ragnarok, Flying Sleeves or Bowling Bash)

    1.  Mechanics

-  Playing Wreath of Flame multiple times will increase the amount of Vigor. It stacks with Akabeko, which also gives Vigor.
-  If an Attack hits multiple times, or hits multiple enemies, Vigor applies to every instance of damage from that Attack.
-  Vigor does not expire at the end of the turn; it is only removed after playing an Attack.
