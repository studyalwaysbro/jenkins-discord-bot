## Artifacts
=Overview=

' are rare and powerful relic items. They are the targets of Salvage Missions. Each type of artifact has an effect on their environment and are sources of rare crafting materials.

Artifacts are kept inside the Artifact Holder in Alien Ruins and sometimes located on the Europan ice walls or sea floor. They require both hands to carry and cannot be put inside the player's inventory or any containers (note that there is a specific Alien artifact container to store the artifacts in), which makes bringing them to the Sub more challenging. (Although this can be circumvented by being dragged by a 2nd player with an Underwater Scooter)

Artifacts can appear as any of 12 formations, made up of several overlapping grey rectangles with circular dents (See image). This kind of formation causes some parts of certain Artifact models to rotate on their own.

An artifact can be identified by hovering the cursor over the item to display its name, or by observing its effects. The Psychosis Alien Artifact has a darker shade of grey compared to the others.

Artifacts have a bright green oval blip on sonar.

=Alien Artifacts=

Five unique types of Alien Artifacts have been recovered from Europa: Faraday, Nasonov, Psychosis, Sky, and Thermal.

    1.  Faraday Alien Artifact

The Faraday Alien Artifact sends out a 2 meter EMP blast every 15 seconds, damaging all Installations within its range, it is recommended that if it's required to transport the artifact for an objective it is kept in an area with no or mostly non-essential installations.

When deconstructed, the output includes 1 Steel Bar and 3 Fulgurium Bars, which can be used to craft Fulgurium Battery Cells and Fulgurium Fuel Rods.

    1.  Nasonov Alien Artifact

The Nasonov Alien Artifact attracts Creatures towards it when being held by the player swimming out in the ocean and when placed in the Submarine. This makes it one of the more dangerous Artifacts as it can cause swarms of Creatures to attack the Submarine simultaneously.

However, the sea Creatures of Europa will always prioritize food over the Artifact. Depending on which Creatures show up first, certain species will attempt to eat smaller preys before focusing on the Submarine. For example, Mantises will prey on Crawlers if they are nearby each other, Tiger Threshers on Mantises, etc.

Regardless, crew members must make haste with the Submarine to safety and avoid sea Creatures as the latter make their way to the Artifact.

When deconstructed, the output includes 1 Steel Bar and 3 icons, which are used to craft icons, Physicorium Ammunition Boxes, and Physicorium Shells.

    1.  Psychosis Alien Artifact

The Psychosis Alien Artifact gives 0.5% Affliction Psychosis every second when being held.

When deconstructed, the output includes 1 Steel Bar and 3 Dementonite Bars, which are used to craft icon.

    1.  Sky Alien Artifact

## Crates
Crates are portable containers. They spawn after items are purchased at a Store, or as part of a Cargo Mission. They come in several different variations. They can themselves be stored in a Crate Shelf.

    1.  Metal Crate
A general storage crate for items bought from the stations, or taken from them. Has a capacity of 20 items. It is not waterproof.

    1.  Chemical Crate
A crate that is supplied when medical supplies are purchased. Has a capacity of 12 items. It is waterproof.

    1.  Explosive Crate
A crate that is supplied when possibly explosive supplies are purchased. Has a capacity of 12 items. It is waterproof.

    1.  Secure Metal Crate
A metal crate with a built in lock that can be set to id cards in the editor but not in game. Has a capacity of 20 items. It is not waterproof.

The Flare is a consumable, throwable item in Barotrauma.

Flares are a single-use item that can be lit, carried, and/or thrown to create a temporary source of light. They can be used in low-light situations such as reactor power-up, power failure, cave diving, and Alien Ruin exploration.

The intended use for the handcuffs are to restrain rogue crew members. They are especially useful in Traitor Mode, as they can be used to detain the traitor.

## Headset
Item description
The Headset is an item used to access radio chat channels.

= Function =
The headset can be used to listen to/speak over Text Radio Chat (Default: R) and Voice Radio Chat (Default: V).

It also requires either a Battery Cell or Fulgurium Battery Cell for power, but doesn't consume it as of v0.10.

Radio channels feature was added in v0.10.5.0. There are 10 thousand and one accessible radio channels (channel 0000 to channel 10000), making it possible to do things like secret communication on another channel or devices that react to commands sent on a specific channel. It also features the Mem option to store the current radio channel as a preset, up to 10 presets from preset 0 to 9, all of which are set to the first 10 channels, channel 0000 to channel 0009, by default. Because the channel number only has 4 digits max, channel 10000 cannot be typed in and is only accessible by switching from channel 9999.

## Mudraptor Egg
The ' is unique item that hatches a Mudraptor.

    1.  Acquisition
The  can be looted 75 6% of the time, bought in a Store, or found inside a Cabinet in a Submarine 1% of the time.

    1.  Function
The Mudraptor Egg can be "fed" a 20 to initiate its growth cycle into a Mudraptor.

The Mudraptor Egg in its small form can be deconstructed into Alien Blood.

    1.  Usage
The  has one inventory slot in it. A 20 can be put in to feed and grow it.

The Egg has three stages of growth, meaning it requires three Salines for each stage. As it slowly consumes the Saline, it also grows in scale little by little.

    1.  Stage 1 (Small)

The small Mudraptor Egg is the default state. It can be held in either hand and stored in the player's inventory or containers. It has a Vitality of 20, therefore it can be damaged and destroyed.

When fed with Saline, the Egg starts consuming 1% of its condition every second and gradually increasing its scale. After consuming 90% of the Saline (90 seconds), the Egg will simultaneously go into its second stage form and become unholdable, falling out of the player's inventory or any containers and permanently anchoring itself on whichever surface it lands on.

    1.  Stage 2 (Medium)

The medium Mudraptor Egg is the middle stage of its growth, having a decent size. It can no longer be picked up but instead acts as a container that only accepts the Saline when interacted with and its Vitality increases to 50.

Feeding the Egg with another Saline will further increase its scale until it changes to its final form. This process also takes 90 seconds.

    1.  Stage 3 (Large)

The large Mudraptor Egg is the final stage of its growth, whose size is visibly huge. Its Vitality significantly increases to 200 in this state.

If the Egg is fed with the third Saline, its scale will continue to further increase. After the duration of 90 seconds, the Egg will hatch and the Mudraptor will burst out. Despite the effort of growing the Egg, the hatched Mudraptor is wild and will attack everyone like any other hostile Mudraptors.

The baby Mudraptor will not grow into a fully sized Mudraptor.

There is a small chance that the icon may burst out along with the Mudraptor as soon as the Egg hatches. It is unknown whether this is intentional or a bug.

    1.  See Also

## Oxygen Tank
The Oxygen Tank is a depletable item that can be found and used in Barotrauma.

    1.  Function
Oxygen tanks are mainly used as an air source when diving underwater. It can be attached to a Diving Suit or Diving Mask to provide the player with oxygen when submerged. They also act as a fuel source for Plasma Cutters.

Oxygen tanks can be refilled when placed inside an Oxygen Generator or an Oxygen Tank Shelf. It can refill a maximum of five tanks at the same time.

If the tank comes into contact with fire, it will explode.

    1.  Usage
When inside an inventory, the oxygen tank can be dragged onto a Diving Mask, Diving Suit, or Plasma Cutter to attach it to the respective item.

While in use the tank will slowly deplete.  Its current condition can be seen under the item in the player's hotbar.

    1.  Damage Values

## Underwater Scooter
0.10.6.2

The Underwater Scooter is a battery-powered underwater propulsion device. It can be used to travel faster while in water, and can also provide light while doing so.

    1.  Function
The underwater scooter can be used to propel oneself through the water faster than swimming can.

Contrary to what some people say, sea creatures will be not be attracted to the noise of the scooter but smaller fish may be attracted to the light. It could happen that larger fish will prey on the small fish but this seldom happens and larger fish, such as sharks, will not  attack divers using it.

At most they will be curious about any noise and nothing more.

It is recommended to bring a scooter on most dives for quicker completion times.

    1.  Usage
To use it, the player can hold the right-click in the desired direction and activate it by then holding the left-click.

The spotlight effect works everywhere, however propulsion can only work when underwater.

Usage requires presence of a charged battery in the container slot.

## Welding Fuel Tank
The Welding Fuel Tank is a depletable item that can be found and used in Barotrauma.

    1.  Function
Welding fuel tank's primary function is (as the name suggests) to be used as a fuel for the Welding Tool.

    1.  Usage
When inside an inventory, the welding fuel tank can be dragged on to a welding tool to attach it. While in use the tank will slowly deplete, the current condition of it can be seen under the item in the player's hotbar.

If the tank comes into contact with fire, it will explode.

Unlike the Oxygen Tank, welding fuel tanks cannot be refilled.

Can also be used to execute / assassinate anyone via putting them in a Diving Suit / putting a Diving Mask on them and replacing the Oxygen Tank with the Welding Fuel Tank. This will kill the victim very quickly.
